# Current pilot: The Man Who Never Ran

This replacement responds to the author's rejection of the previous pilot. Start with **Grand-Chukuchuku_Pilot_v2_Cinematic_Storyboard.pdf**. The HTML viewer offers the same eighteen drawn keyframes with timed dialogue, camera notes and keyboard navigation. The separate screenplay is editable in Markdown and Word.

The delivery contains **18 distinct drawn keyframes and a continuous 36-shot, five-minute editorial plan**. Intermediate animation poses, some reverse-angle coverage, voice performances and a timed animatic are still production work. Five minutes is the target edit, not a measured performance.

The old pilot and its Artlist audio plan remain historical development material. They do not govern this replacement. The new sound design is in the new screenplay and board; no Artlist track has been searched, selected or licensed in this workspace.

## Story and chronology

The pilot opens on an older Zino at the well, moves four years earlier to his age-ten introduction, then returns to the later secondary-school evening that explains the opening. The crowd sequence is a **proposed composite of the witness experiences described by the author**, not a dated reconstruction. Approximate age fourteen / 2004 is a working placement awaiting correction. Friends' exact actions, dialogue, camera type, route and timings are proposed. No accusation is established as guilt.

This standalone pilot condenses moments that the sixty-episode Chapter One develops at greater length. It does not silently rewrite the season into a five-minute chronology. The next short after this pilot must acknowledge its ending rather than present the rejected fee-notice cliffhanger.
