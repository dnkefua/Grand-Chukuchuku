GRAND-CHUKUCHUKU - EPISODE 09 - "THE DECIDING PASS"
SOURCE: Unified 60-Episode Plan v3 (series-development/scripts/Grand-Chukuchuku_Unified_60_Episode_Plan_v3.md), Episode 9. Scene material from the archived Full Shooting Script v2, Ep 05 (SSv2 5).
STATUS: DIALOGUE LOCKED BY THE AUTHOR (21 Nov 2026), ready to record. The author's recording wins where it differs.
AUTHOR NOTES:
- 20 Nov: Zino is already healed. It is a normal day, and he is eager to "chop" another cote.
- 21 Nov: Zino's failed pass hits his OWN bottle in Scene 3. That is how Arey's side reaches two hits, and it is why Gideon calls him "ben-ben foot".
- 21 Nov: Grand Nestor has already split the cote when Ma calls. The teammates are shocked and angry only because Zino bolts.

SETTING: the Fiango road outside the compound, the bottle pitch and Grand Nestor's kiosk.
TIME AND LIGHT: late afternoon, a few days after Episode 8. A low golden sun to start, falling to dusk by the end. The streetlight is dead (SONEL), which is why the game has to finish before dark.
OPENING (aerial rule): a 3 s aerial over the Fiango road and the compound roofs at golden hour, landing on the jeopardy image (Scene 1).

WARDROBE (no-long-pants rule):
- Football players wear shorts and singlets or tees, barefoot or in worn canvas shoes. Zino: white ribbed tank, royal-blue shorts.
- Gideon sits on a crate at the side and does NOT play (green polo, brown trousers).
- Luc walks the verge with his small Bible and does NOT play (mustard tee).
- Grand Nestor: red cap worn backwards, red plaid shirt over a white tee.

SIDES (as in Episodes 6-7): Zino, Ayuk and Robi vs Bismark (captain), Noge and Arey.
TARGETS: "33 Export" bottles (Flow assets), one at each end; slippers as goalposts.
RULE: three hits on the other side's bottle wins the cote. Hitting your own bottle counts for the other side.

CONTINUITY: no crutch and no bandage. A faint pale scar on Zino's right knee. The crutch from Episode 8 leans against the compound wall.
CALLBACK: Episode 5 "Well Water". Carrying water from the well and filling the buckets is Zino's job.
RUNTIME TARGET: about 1:55, within the 2:00 rule.
SUBTITLES: French burned in, low on screen (rule from Ep 9). English is uploaded as a selectable file.

----------------------------------------------------------------
SCENE 1 - COTE DAY (0:00-0:12)
The aerial lands on the compound gate. The crutch leans against the wall. Zino steps out past it without it, flips a coin and catches it.
ZINO: Leg don correct. Today na cote day.

SCENE 2 - THE COTE (0:12-0:32)
Grand Nestor's kiosk. The players drop coins into his upturned cap. Gideon is on his crate; Luc is on the verge.
GRAND NESTOR: Cote don full. Three hit, e done. Who hit three first, chop am.
AREY: Crutch boy don come back? Today you go waka home with empty pocket.
ZINO: Na me go chop this cote. Write am for your head.
SOUND: Gideon's laugh; a bike man rings his bell as he passes.

SCENE 3 - TWO ROUNDS (0:32-0:58)
A quick montage of the game:
- Ayuk hits Arey's side's bottle: 1-0.
- Arey's side answers: 1-1.
- Zino tries to pass to a teammate, mis-hits it, and the ball knocks over HIS OWN bottle: 1-2. Arey's side howls.
GIDEON (laughing): Weh, ben-ben foot!
- Robi hits back: 2-2.
The sun drops behind the roofs and the dead streetlight stays dark.
GIDEON: Zino, sun don go house. Una go play reach morning?
SOUND: laughter SFX on the own bottle (Arey's side and Gideon).

SCENE 4 - THE WRONG HERO (0:58-1:14)  [THE TURN]
Ayuk is open at the better angle. Zino wants the glory and goes for the same feint as in Episode 7. Arey reads it and blocks the angle. Zino's shot misses the bottle and rolls into the gutter. Arey's side erupts.
AYUK: Pass am!
AREY: I don know your touch. Na the same trick every time.
SOUND: laughter from Arey's side (laughter SFX).

SCENE 5 - ONE MORE CHANCE (1:14-1:32)
The ball comes back. Zino is ready to argue. Luc steps in from the verge and catches his wrist before it becomes a quarrel; Luc does not play.
LUC: Use your head, Zino. No play like Etii.
Zino looks at Ayuk, open again. He passes. Ayuk hits the third bottle. It rings and falls: 3-2.
LUC: Na so, my man!
SOUND: the bottle's ring, then the crowd.

SCENE 6 - THE COST (1:32-1:42)
Grand Nestor splits the cote between Zino and Ayuk; Zino's share is in his fist. The boys lift Ayuk, not Zino. Zino smiles, and it costs him.
GIDEON (laughing, repeating his jab): Ben-ben foot!

SCENE 7 - HOOK (1:42-1:55)
Dusk. From inside the compound, Ma calls once, then again, louder:
MA: Ziiiiii! ... Ziiiiii!
- Zino freezes, coins in his fist.
- INSERT: the empty buckets beside the well. He forgot to carry water from the well and fill them.
- INSERT, memory: Ma marching him home by the ear in front of everyone, as she has done before.
- He bolts for home without a word. His teammates are shocked and angry ("Eh!?", arms out, Ayuk kicks the dust).
Hold the last sound over 1 s of black.
TOMORROW TAG (inside the 2:00): the landlord's notebook, open on a table.
END CARD: EPISODE 10 - RENT AND LIGHT
