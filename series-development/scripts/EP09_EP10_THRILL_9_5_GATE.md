## How Episode 9 reaches 9.5 (lines unchanged)

### 1. Land the aerial on Arey, not on the healed leg (+0.5, check 1)

The 3-second aerial still flies over the Fiango road at golden hour. It does not settle on Zino. It settles on Arey at Grand Nestor's cap, already waiting, with the crutch in the same frame against the wall. Then Zino walks past the crutch and says the locked line, "Leg don correct. Today na cote day."

Why this is worth the half point: a cold open is jeopardy, not a location and not a recovery. The crutch is the scar of Episode 8. Arey standing over the money is the threat of Episode 9. One frame can hold both. The coin flip, if it stays, comes after the line, so the audience wants the cote before Zino announces it.

### 2. Show the coin leave his hand, and show the buckets while it is still light (+0.3, check 2)

On Arey's locked line, "Today you go waka home with empty pocket," cut to Zino's coin hitting the other coins in the cap. Hold the cap. That is the stake.

On the walk from the gate to the kiosk, the camera passes the well. The buckets are empty. Nobody speaks. The audience now knows two things he can lose: the cote, and Ma's evening. When he runs at 1:42, it is a choice between those two pictures, not a new idea.

### 3. Make the own-bottle a held shot, not a montage beat (+0.4, check 7)

Scene 3 may stay a montage for the 1–0, 1–1, and 2–2 hits. The 1–2 is not a montage beat. Hold it.

The ball leaves Zino's foot, knocks his own bottle over, and Grand Nestor moves one counting stone from Zino's side to Arey's side. Arey does not celebrate first. He watches the stone move, then he looks at Zino's feet. He is storing the touch. His later line, "I don know your touch. Na the same trick every time," is then a memory, not a boast.

Why this clears the repeat penalty: Episode 7 was "mouth against skill." Episode 9 is "the rival has a file on you, and your mistake scores for him." The stone crossing over is the picture Episode 14 will echo when Arey steps into the feint before Zino starts it.

### 5. The cost has to be visible, and Episode 10 has to inherit it (+0.4, check 6)

Do not play the smile as warmth. Play it as a smile he drops when nobody looks at him. Ayuk is in the air. Zino's share is in a fist at his side. Gideon's second "ben-ben foot" lands after the lift, so the nickname is the last word the road has for him.

Episode 10's first shot must still be that coldness. If Episode 10 opens on a fresh rent scene, this point falls back to 0.6 and Episode 9 cannot hold 9.5, because a cost that the next episode ignores did not carry.

### 6. One hook, one picture (+0.4, check 8)

The last ten seconds are a single frame, not three ideas.

He runs through the compound. In one composition: the empty buckets at the well, the bulb over Ma's table already dead, and the landlord's notebook open on that table. Ma's second "Ziiiiii!" is the sound over the one second of black. The tomorrow tag is that same notebook, closer, three seconds, no new character.

Episode 10's first fifteen seconds answer that frame. He arrives with the coins. The buckets are still empty. The notebook is still open. The landlord is already in the doorway because 7 p.m. has come. The water, the dark, and the rent are one evening, which is why the hook is allowed to contain all three.

### 7. The clock has to force the run (+0.5, check 4)

Gideon's sun line is the warning. The forcing picture is later, and it has to sit inside the last 30 seconds.

When Ma calls, he is facing the celebration. He turns and the well is a black disc. The buckets are what he sees. He cannot fetch four buckets and still stand with his team. Dark has already made the job late. That is the choice, and it is visible: team in the last light on the road, well in the dark, coins in his hand.

The dead streetlight should be in the 2–2 shot, not only in the header, so the audience has seen the clock before it closes.

## Episode 10 — why the one-line plan scores about 7.0, and the draft scores 9.5

The v3 line is: Ma's pay is late, SONEL has cut the lights, the landlord is at the door at 7 p.m., Zino's bottle money can cover the shortfall but confesses the gambling, he chooses Ma, she puts the cane on the table.

That line already has an opponent, a turn, a cost, and a hook. It does not yet have:

- a cold open on jeopardy (the notebook is named, but the episode could still open on a dark room as mood);
- a clock inside the last 30 seconds (7 p.m. is a fact in the header; it has to be a deadline he can miss on screen);
- a season loop that moves (the row says loop E, but Arey does not appear in the one-line version);
- a hook Episode 11 can answer (a cane on a table asks for a punishment morning; Episode 11 is a camera gig).

The draft below keeps the author's turn (he gives up the secret to save the evening) and adds the missing pictures.

### What each addition is for

**Open on the notebook in the landlord's hand, under a dead bulb.** Check 1. Three seconds. The aerial of the dark compound lands on that hand. Rent night has started before Zino arrives, so he is late for a clock that is already running.

**He still has the road on him.** Check 6, carried from Episode 9. Ayuk does not greet him. Someone on the verge repeats "ben-ben foot" once, off screen, no new story. The coins in his fist are the same coins Nestor counted out. The audience must recognise the fist from the end of Episode 9.

**The shortfall is the size of the fist, and no number is spoken.** Check 2. The landlord's finger stops on a line. Zino looks at his own hand. The audience sees that the hidden cote matches the gap. Inventing a franc amount would break the fact rule. The match is visual.

**Arey is advanced without being in the room.** Check 7. Gideon, from the window, one line: Arey is on the road telling people Zino ran with the cote. Loop E moves. Arey has turned the bolt into a public story. That is the higher stake Episode 14 collects. It also means Zino is not only hiding the gambling from Ma. The road already knows.

**7 p.m. is counted down in the last 30 seconds.** Check 4. Early on, the landlord says he finishes the round at seven. A wall clock or his own watch is visible. In the last half-minute he starts to close the notebook. Zino has to open his hand before the book shuts. The clock is the book closing, which the audience can see.

**The turn is the hand opening, between 0:55 and 1:10.** Check 5. Until then his plan is to keep the fist in his pocket and let Ma face the landlord. The plan fails when the closing book and the size of the gap meet. He opens the hand. Ma sees the cote and the rescue in the same second.

**The cost is the cane and the silence, and both survive until Episode 11.** Check 6. She takes the coins. She does not thank him. She pays the landlord enough to leave tonight. She puts the cane on the table and says "Tomorrow." Episode 11 must begin with that cane still on the table, or with the morning that follows it, before the gig starts. If Episode 11 opens on a party, this point is lost.

**The hook is the gig, not a new mystery.** Check 8. The cane is the cost. The hook is Gideon at the window after the landlord leaves: one roll of film, a borrowed camera, he needs Zino's mouth in the morning. Episode 11's first fifteen seconds answer it: the cane is dealt with in one shot, then the borrowed camera and the deposit. The tomorrow tag is the girl from Episode 8 turning her face out of a lens. No explanation. Episode 11's party scene is where that image pays.

The girl is not named, not called the discipline master's daughter, and not connected to the water rumour. Episode 19 is still the shared shock.
