# GRAND-CHUKUCHUKU

## Episode 01 — “Grand-Chukuchuku Never Loses”

**Target length:** 2 minutes 20 seconds
**Format:** Vertical 9:16 animated short
**Language:** English and Cameroon Pidgin, with English subtitles
**Draft status:** Revision 2 — proposed/composite scene arrangement

### What this episode establishes

Zino is ten, around 2000, after completing primary school. He is brilliant, funny, competitive, and determined to seem larger than the pressures around him. Fiango is a community full of life, but money is always close by: the landlord comes for rent and electricity contributions, and Zino's mother works as a primary-school teacher.

This episode does **not** assign a date to the school-fee exclusion. Zino's father died when he was two, so that family history belongs in a prologue or testimony scene rather than Zino's own flashback. It does not show jungle justice; that material has its own consequence-led secondary-school episode.

---

### 0:00–0:18 — COLD OPEN: FIANGO IS ALIVE

**WIDE.** A ball hits red dirt. Houses form a close circle around the compound. A bucket rests by the well. The mango tree throws shade across students, workers, and children moving through the shared space.

**ON-SCREEN TEXT:** FIANGO, KUMBA — AROUND 2000

**ZINO (V.O.):** In Fiango, everybody knows your business before you know it yourself.

The football cuts between feet. **ZINO** takes possession. **GIDEON** yells for a pass. **LUC** calls a foul before anyone falls.

**LUC:** Zino, pass the ball. Even Moses passed something.

**ZINO:** Moses no get my left foot.

### 0:18–0:38 — GRAND-CHUKUCHUKU ARRIVES

Zino chips the ball. It lands in the branches of the mango tree.

**GIDEON:** Your left foot don carry ball go rent house upstairs.

**ZINO:** Grand-Chukuchuku once score from there.

**FANTASY CUT.** The same compound stretches into a vast stadium. GRAND-CHUKUCHUKU leaps from the tree, volleys the ball through a goal that appears in the sky, and lands without dust on his shoes.

**GIDEON (O.S.):** Zino! Bring down our ball!

The fantasy snaps away. Zino looks up at the stuck ball, suddenly very ordinary.

### 0:38–1:03 — THE LANDLORD

The LANDLORD enters the compound carrying a small notebook. Doors become quieter. Zino's mother, returning from primary school with exercise books under one arm, sees him.

**LANDLORD:** Rent and light bill. I go come back before dark.

Zino hears it. Gideon whispers:

**GIDEON:** Grand-Chukuchuku fit pay light bill too?

Zino wants to fire back. He sees his mother forcing a calm smile.

**ZINO:** He can pay for the whole town.

**LUC:** Then make e start with our ball.

Zino climbs for the ball. The others laugh, but his face changes as he watches the landlord leave.

### 1:03–1:33 — WHAT HIS MOTHER SEES

**INT. ZINO'S HOME — LATER.** Zino enters, schoolwork open on a table. His mother marks primary-school exercise books. Through the doorway, the landlord's notebook is visible in the yard.

**MOTHER:** Homework first. Football after.

**ZINO:** I was doing advanced football mathematics.

She almost laughs. He waits.

**ZINO:** Mum... Grand-Chukuchuku says one day he will build a house with light that never goes off.

**MOTHER:** Grand-Chukuchuku talks plenty.

**ZINO:** He is a man of action.

**MOTHER:** Good. Then you too—do your action. Finish that sum.

### 1:33–2:02 — A REAL WIN

Zino works. A difficult maths problem turns into a small animated football formation in his imagination. He erases, thinks, solves it.

His mother checks the answer. Nods, proud but not sentimental.

**MOTHER:** Correct.

For Zino, it feels like a stadium roaring. A flash of GRAND-CHUKUCHUKU applauding from the mango tree.

### 2:02–2:20 — BUTTON / CLIFFHANGER

A knock at the door. The landlord's shadow crosses the entrance.

Zino looks at his mother. She closes the exercise book.

**MOTHER:** Sit down. Let me talk.

Zino sits with the completed sum in front of him. Outside, football resumes. Inside, he listens to adult voices too low to understand.

**ZINO (V.O.):** Grand-Chukuchuku never loses. Me, I was still learning what losing costs.

**CUT TO BLACK.**

**END CARD:** GRAND-CHUKUCHUKU — EPISODE 02: “RENT AND LIGHT”
