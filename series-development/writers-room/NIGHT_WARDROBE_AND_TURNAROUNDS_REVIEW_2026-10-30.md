## The correction that changes the scene

Professional School's evening revision is a voluntary space in regular house/casual clothing. Some students dress to impress. There are no student uniforms at night. The matching sky-blue/navy clothes in the earlier pilot sheets were therefore a continuity error; the author's later correction overrides them. The same sky-blue/navy uniform remains correct for daytime Forms One–Five. Boarding-school colours are still unconfirmed.

The proposed pilot clothes distinguish three intentions without dialogue: Zino's plain burgundy T-shirt and charcoal trousers; Gideon's forest-green polo, dark-brown trousers and camera bag; Luc's mustard T-shirt, loose navy casual trousers and small Bible. These are proposed garment choices, not remembered exact outfits. They must persist from night revision through the crowd and the return to the well, including the cold open's repeated moment. They do not become the only clothes each character ever wears.

## What the turnarounds must establish

The requested views are front, left side and back, all full-body and at consistent scale. A three-view sheet is a design reference; it is not an animated rotation, a complete age progression, facial-expression library or production rig. The later-secondary age of approximately fourteen–fifteen follows the working chronology and must not be silently applied to the age-ten passage.

Zino's handsome face and clean fade, Luc's high flat-top, trained build, large hands and ears, and Gideon's larger head, prominent nose, small front-tooth gap and fuller W-shaped hairline are the supplied distinguishing traits. Mirabel's slim build and Lizzy's pretty face receive ordinary school-age proportions, clothes and neutral poses. The girls remain distinct from provisional Elianne. Exact girls' hair and casual wardrobes are proposed choices. Canonical spellings remain **Gideon** and **Mirabel**; the latest variants do not create new people.
