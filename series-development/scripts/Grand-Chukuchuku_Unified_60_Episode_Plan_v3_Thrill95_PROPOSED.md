**Raised beyond 8.5 to 9.5:**
1. **Three season-long mysteries** run through all 60 episodes, so every episode touches at least one open question.
2. **Dramatic irony:** the audience learns the girl is the discipline master's daughter **eight episodes before Zino does** (planted in Ep 11, discovered by Zino in Ep 19). Every scene with her in between plays like a ticking bomb.
3. **Two antagonist ladders** escalate across the season instead of resetting every episode:
   - **Arey** at the bottles and on the street;
   - **the big boy** at school: he tore the page in Ep 4, and in Ep 21 he gives it back with Zino's name added.
4. **A cost rule:** every episode takes something from Zino that does not come back the next day (money, trust, an object, a friendship, face).
5. **A mirror structure:** in Ep 16 the crowd spares Grand K; in Eps 33–35 a crowd does not spare. Act III's horror lands because Act II showed mercy.

## 2. Thrill 9.5 rubric (checked at the script gate)

| # | Check | Points |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Cold open in the first 3 seconds:** an image of jeopardy, not a location card. After an aerial opening, the jeopardy image is the very next shot. | 1.0 |
| 2 | **Stake on screen:** something Zino can visibly lose (coin, camera, seat, badge, Ma's trust, a friend). | 1.5 |
| 3 | **Named opposition:** a person, an institution or a clock with a face (Arey, the big boy, the DM, the landlord, the bell, the fee notice). | 1.0 |
| 4 | **A visible clock** that forces a choice in the last 30 seconds. | 1.5 |
| 5 | **A turn between 0:55 and 1:10:** the plan fails, a helper becomes the problem, or the truth flips. | 1.5 |
| 6 | **A cost** that carries into the next episode. | 1.0 |
| 7 | **A season loop touched:** mystery or irony advanced, never only mentioned. | 1.0 |
| 8 | **A hook in the last 10 seconds** that the next episode's first 15 seconds answer or worsen. | 1.0 |

- **9.5** means all eight checks are met, with at most 0.5 lost on execution.
- **Penalty:** an engine already used in an earlier episode costs 1.0 unless the stakes are higher **and** the antagonist has learned something since.
