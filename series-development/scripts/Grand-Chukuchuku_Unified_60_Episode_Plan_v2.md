## Settled canon (16 Nov)
- **Five years.** Act I is Form 1 (1999–2000), Act II is Forms 2–3 (2000–2002) and Act III is Forms 4–5 (2002–2004), ending at the O-Levels. The boys age on screen: older model sheets are needed at Episode 13 and Episode 25.
- **The three friends:** Zino, Luc and Gideon.
- **Grand Chuckuchucku** is Zino's own invention, born from the legend of Grand Kumba. Episode 5 is the first time the name is told.
- **Backstories:**
  - Zino was born in Kumba; the family moved to Fiango so Ma is near the school where she teaches.
  - Gideon came from Bamenda when his elder brother became a lawyer at the Kumba High Court.
  - Luc came from Mamfe when his father opened a store.
- **Grand Kumba:** a thief who stole from corrupt officials and gave half to the people. He was never beaten when caught, and later headed the market tax collection.
- **Mami Water:** always a rumour, never shown as true or false. Schoolgirls are never sexualised.
- **Lake Barombi, RASKA week and Victor Fish:** drownings are seen from the shore; no body is shown.

## Open decision: the Episode 8 slot
The plan puts "The Deciding Pass" at 8 and "The Message" at 11. Production is currently making "The Message" as Episode 8. The author needs to choose between:
- moving "The Message" up to 8, with "The Deciding Pass", "Rent and Light" and "Gideon's First Gig" each moving back one; or
- renumbering "The Message" as Episode 11.

## Standing rules
- No narration voice-over.
- No students live in the compound except Zino. Gideon (1A) and Luc (1C) never sit in Form 1B.
- Every episode ends on an open question that the next episode's first scene answers or worsens.
- Every script states, before recording:
  - the time of day and the light for each scene;
  - how the first 15 seconds pick up from the last episode;
  - the closing question;
  - why each character is in each scene;
  - a thriller score out of 10 (rewrite anything under 6).
