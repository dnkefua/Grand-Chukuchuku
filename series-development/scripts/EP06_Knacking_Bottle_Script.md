# GRAND-CHUKUCHUKU
## EPISODE 06 — “KNACKING BOTTLE”
**Target Runtime:** 1:45 – 2:00  
**Format:** Vertical 9:16 (cinematic 2D adult animation)  
**Setting:** Fiango dirt road, hot afternoon sun, red dust, mango trees, zinc-roof houses  
**Cast:** Zino, Ayuk, Bismark (Team 1) vs. Arey, Noge, Robi (Team 2) | Sideline: Gideon, Luc  

---

### ACT I — THE COTE ON THE DIRT (0:00 – 0:30)

**EXT. FIANGO DIRT ROAD — AFTERNOON**

**VISUAL:**  
Low-angle macro shot on the baked red road outside the compound. An empty brown glass beer bottle ("33" Export) stands upright on dry, cracked clay in the foreground.  
A camera whip-pan reveals a second beer bottle standing exactly **15 meters away** down the unpaved street. The road surface between them is clear red clay and gravel for unrestricted 360-degree play.

Off to the side of the road, on an upturned wooden crate by the verandah where Gideon sits, sits a metal tin cup (or held safely by a bystander outside the playing boundary). Six teenage boys in dusty shorts step to the sideline to drop their 100 CFA franc coins into the cup: **600 CFA total pot held safely off the pitch**.

Zino wipes sweat from his fresh fade haircut, tightening the laces of his scuffed canvas shoes.

**GIDEON** *(lounging on a wooden crate at the sideline, chewing a stick)*:  
You sure say your 100 francs want play today, Zino? Bottle no dey get mercy for school prefect oh! Once e knack, no refund!

**ZINO** *(smirking, tapping his shoe on the dirt)*:  
My 100 francs get ambition. Six of we don put money. Winning team carry 600, each man take 200. Three knacks. All man inside.

**AREY** *(opposing team captain, cracking his knuckles)*:  
Talk plenty, Zino. Today your knee go kiss gravel.

---

### ACT II — NO OFFSIDE & THE BEHIND-THE-BOTTLE AMBUSH (0:30 – 0:55)

**VISUAL:**  
The scuffed leather ball is kicked off. Instant high-octane frenzy. Red dust kicks up in rooster tails.  
Because there is **no offside**, Arey immediately sprints *behind* Zino's team's beer bottle, crouching and waiting for an acute rebound strike.

**AREY** *(calling out from behind the bottle)*:  
Pass am behind! Bottle no get back or front!

**AYUK** *(Zino's teammate, diving low in front of the bottle like a street goalkeeper)*:  
Zino, watch your rear! Cover the glass!

Zino tracks back, intercepts a bullet pass with his chest, and turns 180 degrees in one fluid motion.

---

### ACT III — INSANE DRIBBLE & BLOODY SLIDING TACKLE (0:55 – 1:20)

**VISUAL:**  
Zino accelerates down the dirt road. Facing two defenders, he pulls off a breathtaking street elastico nutmeg, snapping the ball between Robi's legs. 

He winds up for a direct rocket shot at the far bottle.  
Defender Noge launches a desperate, bone-crunching **sliding tackle** straight across the coarse red gravel.

**SFX:** Harsh friction `SKRRRR-CLAT!`, dust plume exploding.

Zino is clipped mid-stride. He skids violently on the abrasive dirt road.  
**CLOSE-UP:** Raw, bleeding scratches rip across Zino's knees and right shin. Crimson blood beads against the red dust and embedded gravel.  
Without missing a beat, Zino pushes himself up, teeth gritted, veins pulsing.

**ZINO:**  
You come play ball or you come scrape my skin?!

---

### ACT IV — THE KNACK! (1:20 – 1:35)

**VISUAL:**  
Ayuk recovers the loose ball and squares it back to Zino.  
Zino fakes left, spins, and unleashes a low-trajectory laser strike across the dirt.

**SFX & VISUAL (SLOW MOTION):**  
The ball clips the opposing beer bottle squarely on its amber glass neck!  
`CLINK-CLACK!` — The bottle spins violently on its base, wobbles, and topples flat into the dust!

**ZINO & AYUK** *(roaring in celebration, raising index fingers)*:  
GOAL! ONE KNACK! ONE KNACK DON ENTER! GIMME MY MONEY!

---

### ACT V — THE DISPUTE & MARTIAL ARTS BRAWL (1:35 – 2:00)

**VISUAL:**  
Arey storms in, blocking Zino from reaching the handkerchief of coins.

**AREY:**  
Which goal?! E touch stone for ground before bottle! That one no count!

**ZINO:**  
You dey blind?! The bottle sing like church bell! Hand over the 600 francs!

**LUC** *(running in from the roadside, Bible in one hand, waving his arms)*:  
Stop! Stop am! Bible say blessed are the peacemakers! No turn 100 francs game to war!

**AREY:**  
Comot for road, Pastor!

Arey throws a wild hook. Zino ducks cleanly underneath in an anime-style sway, pivots on his bloody foot, and launches a **jaw-dropping flying karate dropkick** through the swirling red dust cloud!

**SFX:** `WHOOOSH — POW!`

Pandemonium erupts: sweeping leg kicks, mid-air martial arts blocks, and flying dust as the two teams clash in a full martial arts street brawl!

**GIDEON** *(jumping up on his crate, howling with laughter)*:  
YEE-HAA! Bruce Lee don enter Fiango road! Knock am, Zino!

In the scuffle, a stray kick sends the beer bottle flying into a concrete pillar—`SMASH!`  
Suddenly, a commanding woman's voice echoes from the compound gate:

**MOTHER (O.S., booming English):**  
**ZINO! ARE YOU PEOPLE CRAZY OUT THERE?!**

**BEAT:**  
Instant silence. The boys freeze mid-strike.

**GIDEON:**  
Scatter! Mami don land!

All six boys bolt in opposite directions into the alleys, leaving the red dirt settling over the soccer ball, broken glass, and the 600 CFA coins glinting in the dusk.

**END CARD: *EPISODE 07***
