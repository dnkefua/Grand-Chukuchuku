# Artlist audio storyboard handoff

`Grand-Chukuchuku_Chapter_01_Pilot_Artlist_Audio_Storyboard_v1.pdf` is the music-and-sound companion to the five-minute visual pilot board. It maps all 17 pilot boards to Artlist search briefs and edit points without inventing track titles or claiming that a licence has been obtained.

Your Artlist account may be connected in ChatGPT, but there is currently no callable Artlist connector or authenticated account route exposed to this environment. Open the account, use the search briefs, then record the selected track, artist, version and licence information in the final production folder.

The visual storyboard remains at `../Grand-Chukuchuku_Chapter_01_Pilot_Visual_Storyboard.pdf`.
