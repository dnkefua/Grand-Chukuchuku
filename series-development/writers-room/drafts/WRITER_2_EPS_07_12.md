# EPISODE 07 — “PROFESSIONAL SCHOOL”

**Target runtime:** 2:30  
**Fact tier:** Confirmed setting; proposed scene construction and dialogue.  
**Logline:** During June exam season, Zino discovers that night access to Professional School can make him more serious—or simply give him a better place to hide from home.

### 0:00–0:20 — DAY BECOMES NIGHT

**VERTICAL ESTABLISHER.** Late sun disappears over a real vocational campus: empty construction bays, electrical-training rooms, workshop doors locked for the day. Secondary-school students begin filtering toward the open classrooms.

**ON-SCREEN TEXT:** JUNE. EXAM SEASON.

**ZINO (V.O., PROPOSED):** In daytime, that place teach people how to build things. At night, we came there to build our own futures. Or to waste them.

Zino meets GIDEON and LUC at the gate. One group carries revision books. Another arrives laughing, with no book visible. Luc gives the laughing group a disapproving look.

**LUC (PROPOSED):** Book first. Enjoyment can wait.

**GIDEON (PROPOSED):** Enjoyment no dey wait for anybody.

Zino grins and enters between them.

### 0:20–1:15 — FOUR ROOMS

**RAPID VERTICAL PASSES.**

Room one: students silently revise by a weak classroom light. Zino recognizes a difficult question and sits with them.

Room two: a study group debates an answer. Zino gives a sharp answer, then looks pleased when heads turn.

Room three: students flirt in the corridor; smoke moves through a window. Gideon uses Zino as an introduction to someone he wants to impress.

**GIDEON (PROPOSED, LOW):** Talk for me.

**ZINO (PROPOSED):** I fit start the conversation. You go finish am.

Room four: a student sleeps over an open notebook. Another watches the hallway rather than studying.

Zino stops between the rooms. GRAND-CHUKUCHUKU appears beside him in fantasy, wearing a scholar's robe and a footballer's boots. He points toward every door at once.

**GRAND-CHUKUCHUKU (FANTASY, PROPOSED):** A real man passes all the rooms.

Zino's smile fades. He returns to the study room.

### 1:15–2:05 — THE CHOICE

Luc struggles with a maths question. Zino solves part of it, teasing him as he works.

**ZINO (PROPOSED):** You want save everybody, but this number don save itself from you.

Luc laughs despite himself, then turns serious.

**LUC (PROPOSED):** You dey joke because you no want anybody know when something dey worry you.

Zino looks toward the corridor, where Gideon waves him over. He looks at his notes, then at Luc.

**SOUND:** A distant burst of shouting outside the campus. Students freeze for half a beat, then keep moving.

Zino closes his book, unsettled.

### 2:05–2:30 — HOOK

The three friends step into the corridor. More voices rise beyond the gate. A student runs past them toward the sound.

**GIDEON (PROPOSED):** Wetin happen?

**LUC (PROPOSED):** Leave it.

Zino is already looking toward the gate.

**CUT TO BLACK.**

**END CARD:** EPISODE 08 — “THE CROWD”

**Writer's note:** The nightlife, dialogue, exact question, and noise placement are proposed. Professional School's daytime vocational function and night classroom access are confirmed.

# EPISODE 08 — “THE CROWD”

**Target runtime:** 2:40  
**Fact tier:** Confirmed that Zino witnessed at least four jungle-justice incidents in Fiango during day school; proposed composite event, dialogue, and individual action.  
**Logline:** An accusation turns into public violence before Zino, Gideon, and Luc can decide whether to look away.

### 0:00–0:25 — AT THE GATE

The three boys leave Professional School with other students. A crowd pours through the road outside. Adults and students shout over one another.

**MULTIPLE VOICES (PROPOSED):** Thief! / Hold am! / No let e run!

Zino tries to see. Luc grabs his sleeve.

**LUC (PROPOSED):** Zino. No go there.

**ZINO (PROPOSED):** Everybody dey go there.

**LUC:** That no make am right.

Gideon pulls out his camera, then hesitates.

### 0:25–1:35 — THE ATTACK

The crowd opens for a second. An **UNNAMED ACCUSED MAN** is pulled down. The accusation is loud, but no evidence is shown. Zino cannot hear the man's explanation over the crowd.

**HARD-R WITNESS VIEW.** Boots strike. The man raises an arm against blows. Four tires are forced around him. Fuel splashes the road and his clothing. The smell hits the students before the flame does.

Luc grips his small Bible, unable to speak. Gideon brings the camera halfway up, then lowers it as the fire catches. Zino is locked in place. The man screams. Heat rolls toward the children watching at the edge of the crowd.

For a second GRAND-CHUKUCHUKU appears in Zino's imagination, towering above the crowd with one hand raised to stop it. He opens his mouth—but no voice comes out. The fantasy fractures and vanishes.

**ZINO (V.O., PROPOSED):** Everybody knew the word they were shouting. Nobody knew the whole story.

### 1:35–2:15 — AFTER THE CROWD

The crowd moves back, leaving smoke, silence, and people who cannot meet each other's eyes. Zino turns away and nearly stumbles. Gideon holds the unused camera against his chest. Luc closes the Bible.

**GIDEON (PROPOSED):** I no fit take that picture.

**LUC (PROPOSED):** I no fit pray fast enough.

Zino says nothing.

### 2:15–2:40 — HOME

At the compound, Zino's mother sees the smoke in his uniform and the change in his face.

**MOTHER (PROPOSED):** Sit down.

Zino starts a Grand-Chukuchuku story, but stops after one sentence.

**ZINO (PROPOSED):** Grand-Chukuchuku for—

Nothing follows.

**CUT TO BLACK.**

**END CARD:** EPISODE 09 — “RESULTS ON AIR”

**Writer's note:** The hard-R violence is direct by author instruction. The accused person's guilt remains unknown; the scene must never imply that accusation proves guilt.

# EPISODE 09 — “RESULTS ON AIR”

**Target runtime:** 2:35  
**Fact tier:** Confirmed academic milestone, radio results, uncle's promise, and boarding-school choice; proposed dialogue, result-day arrangement, and transition from Episode 8.  
**Logline:** Zino turns back to study after violence shakes his faith in control, then hears his Ordinary-Level result on the radio and receives an uncle's promise that changes his future.

### 0:00–0:35 — TEN SUBJECTS

Zino sits with exam materials. The sound of the earlier crowd returns as a sharp audio flash, then gives way to pencil scratches, a chemistry diagram, and the football outside.

**ON-SCREEN TEXT:** TEN ORDINARY-LEVEL SUBJECTS. MAXIMUM: ELEVEN.

Miss Mukete passes Zino's desk. She does not comfort him; she points to an unfinished answer.

**MISS MUKETE (PROPOSED):** You know this. Finish it.

Zino does. MONTAGE: exercise books, Professional School at night, his mother marking primary-school books, Gideon with a camera, Luc studying with his Bible nearby.

### 0:35–1:25 — THE RADIO WAIT

Time passes without an invented grade or exam result. People gather around a radio at a location to be supplied by Zino. The radio hisses. Every name before Zino's makes the silence heavier.

**SOUND:** ANNOUNCER'S VOICE, indistinct at first. A name. A burst of celebration outside frame. Then more static.

Zino watches his mother. She does not tell him to be calm. She simply listens.

### 1:25–2:05 — HIS NAME

The announcer calls Zino's name.

The exact celebration remains to be supplied: laughter, church, food, music, tears, a run through the compound, or quiet relief. In this proposed construction, his uncle steps forward after the sound of celebration settles.

**UNCLE (PROPOSED—REPLACE WITH REMEMBERED WORDS):** You make me proud. Choose the school you want. Make all five Advanced-Level papers, and America go follow.

Zino hears only one word: America.

### 2:05–2:35 — THE DECISION

GRAND-CHUKUCHUKU crosses mountains in one stride, carrying a suitcase toward an American skyline. The fantasy turns into a real road map of Cameroon. Zino points to the boarding-school option.

**ZINO (PROPOSED):** I go do am.

His mother looks at him with pride and the knowledge that this choice takes him farther away.

**CUT TO BLACK.**

# EPISODE 10 — “THE LONG ROAD”

**Target runtime:** 2:30  
**Fact tier:** Confirmed destination type, day-long travel, mountains/valleys, and boarding-school environment; proposed travel, arrival, and dialogue.  
**Logline:** Zino leaves Fiango for the school he chose to earn an uncle's promise, only to discover a world where distance, wealth, discipline, and bullying set the rules.

### 0:00–0:35 — LEAVING FIANGO

The compound wakes around Zino's packed belongings. The well, mango tree, and football are framed one last time. His mother checks his things with a teacher's practical eye.

**MOTHER (PROPOSED):** You choose this school. Now choose your work too.

Zino performs confidence. GRAND-CHUKUCHUKU strides over a mountain with a suitcase, then snaps back into Zino carrying ordinary bags.

### 0:35–1:25 — THE ROAD

**TRAVEL MONTAGE.** Do not specify bus, car, or companions until Zino supplies them. Roads become slopes. The world opens into mountains and valleys. The trip lasts a day.

**ZINO (V.O., PROPOSED):** From Fiango, every road feel like it go somewhere you know. This one kept going.

As the boarding school appears, Zino sees polished luggage, confident students, and parents who appear wealthier than his household. White teachers and priests cross the grounds with practiced authority. The scale impresses and intimidates him.

### 1:25–2:10 — FIRST LOOK AT THE RULES

At the gate, students fall into lines. An older student watches newcomers. A bell cuts through the air. No specific rule or bully action is invented yet, but Zino notices quickly that people here know how to rank one another.

**OLDER STUDENT (PROPOSED):** New boy, you go learn.

Zino's temper rises. He swallows the answer he wants to give.

### 2:10–2:30 — THE FENCE

Night. From a dormitory window, Zino sees the fence around the school grounds.

**ZINO (V.O., PROPOSED):** The fence look small from far. Everything look small from far.

# EPISODE 11 — “THE FENCE”

**Target runtime:** 2:30  
**Fact tier:** Confirmed that Zino witnessed students punished after jumping the boarding-school fence to buy food; proposed event arrangement, dialogue, and particular punishment.  
**Logline:** In a school built on discipline, Zino learns that hunger can turn an ordinary fence into a test of fear, authority, and survival.

### 0:00–0:35 — WHAT THE FENCE MEANS

Morning routine: bell, line, dormitory beds, uniforms, rules. Zino watches students glance toward the fence. He overhears talk about buying food outside, but no individual student is named.

**STUDENT (PROPOSED, WHISPERED):** For outside, you fit buy something better.

**ZINO (PROPOSED):** And for inside?

The student gives no answer.

### 0:35–1:30 — CAUGHT

At a later time, a small group is brought back inside after crossing the fence to buy food. Zino is not among them. The school authority's words and the exact punishment must come from Zino's memory before production; in this hard-R proposed construction, the discipline is shown as physically and emotionally severe without claiming a precise historic procedure.

Students are made to watch. The punished students struggle to stay composed. The dormitory goes silent. Zino's anger rises, but he sees how little power he has in the room.

**ZINO (V.O., PROPOSED):** In Fiango, the crowd could become a law. Here, the rulebook could become one too.

### 1:30–2:10 — BULLYING AND SILENCE

That night, older students use the punishment as a warning to newer boys. The content of the bullying remains to be supplied; show Zino being tested without inventing an assault. He holds his temper because he understands that reaction can make him a target.

### 2:10–2:30 — THE NEW RULE

Zino sits on his bed with an exercise book, listening to hunger and fear move through the dormitory.

**ZINO (PROPOSED):** I go pass this place. But I no go forget am.

# EPISODE 12 — “ADVANCED LEVELS”

**Target runtime:** 2:45  
**Fact tier:** Confirmed five Advanced-Level subjects, 100% school pass rate, radio results, and uncle's deferred-America condition; proposed dialogue, celebration, and final image.  
**Logline:** Zino completes the five Advanced-Level subjects that should unlock America, only to learn that meeting a promise's condition does not mean the promise arrives.

### 0:00–0:40 — FIVE SUBJECTS

**ON-SCREEN TEXT:** FIVE ADVANCED-LEVEL SUBJECTS. MAXIMUM: FIVE.

The boarding-school years compress into images with altered meaning: the mountain road, rigid line-ups, the fence, a classroom, a priest passing a corridor, a wealthy parent collecting a student, Zino studying alone after lights out. He writes each paper.

**ZINO (V.O., PROPOSED):** Five papers. That was the road they put in front of me.

### 0:40–1:35 — ONE HUNDRED PERCENT

Students gather around a radio. The school has a 100% Advanced-Level pass rate, so the tension is not fear of failure. It is the charged pride of waiting for names, proof, and release.

**SOUND:** RADIO STATIC. ANNOUNCER. A name. Cheers. Another name. More cheers.

Zino's name is read. His reaction and the exact people present must come from his memory. In this proposed staging, his face first goes still, then breaks into joy as the school erupts around him.

### 1:35–2:15 — THE REMINDER

After celebration, Zino reminds his uncle of the Ordinary-Level promise. Do not use this dialogue as a factual quote.

**ZINO (PROPOSED):** Uncle, I do the five.

**UNCLE (PROPOSED—REPLACE WITH REMEMBERED WORDS):** First finish your university degree. Then America.

The celebration noise remains audible, but it now feels farther away. Zino has fulfilled the condition; the condition has moved.

### 2:15–2:45 — THE NEXT DREAM

Zino returns to the compound or another location confirmed by his memory. His mother sees the result. The words between them remain open. Grand-Chukuchuku stands at the edge of the frame, no longer invincible or useful.

Zino folds the result into a book, beside a blank university page or an implied path not yet named.

**ZINO (V.O., PROPOSED):** I pass the five. Now I had to pass the next life.
