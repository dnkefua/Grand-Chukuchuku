## What changed enough to pass author review

Trembling hands and an unanswered question replace a casual football argument. The narrative returns to the same moment after showing its cause. This is suspense with an actual payoff, rather than a random image of danger or an unexplained bill.

Zino's invented rule—Grand never runs—comes into conflict with a real responsibility: get both friends away from the crowd. Leaving is a choice, not an action-film triumph. His final “There was a man” begins testimony instead of another impossible rescue story. That is a behavioural change earned by the preceding event.

### Fiango has functions beyond atmosphere

The vocational school's teaching diagrams remain visible during night revision; the boys have mathematics, camera work, faith and social attention before the threat. The mango tree and well return as stable home landmarks. Cultural details produce actions rather than appearing as a checklist.

### The danger has a truthful boundary

The accusation does not establish guilt. No evidence photograph, secret pursuer, kidnapping, police chase, conspiracy or impossible childhood rescue is manufactured. The final third-sheet repair makes the fire read as violence against a human being rather than an unexplained tire bonfire. The witness stays anonymous and the production notes label the sequence as a proposed composite.

## Findings raised during this review

### Corrected in the replacement

1. **Age-ten figures initially had adult-looking facial hair.** Revised first-sheet faces are clean-shaven and child-readable. The older reaction close-ups on the final third sheet also read younger after revision. A full turnaround/model pass is still needed; one keyframe does not lock every age.
2. **The rupture image initially risked reading as a tire fire without a human victim.** The final sheet includes the anonymous adult's raised forearm among the tires; the preceding restrained-adult POV provides the causal context. It no longer depends solely on a caption claiming harm that the image never suggests.
3. **Water staging risked implying that Zino washed soot into the communal well.** Canonical screenplay and data now explicitly describe fresh water lifted from a drawn bucket beside the well, with trembling preventing him from bringing it to his mouth. The hand keyframes themselves are ambiguous; the wider production layout must show that bucket. They must not be used as visual authority for a washing action.
4. **The old pilot's money continuity and primary-school age conflict have been removed.** No betting pot must be accounted for in this version, and no confirmed 1999 primary exclusion is moved into the age-ten present.
5. **Grand's later imaginary intervention has been removed.** He enters through an active tale and does not appear in the real crowd. His absence is a formal contrast, not another magical effect.
6. **K14 initially claimed an empty-roof/deep-background composition that was not drawn.** The canonical metadata and rebuilt PDF now identify it as a tight reaction with the separate empty-roof insert still pending. The shot table explicitly labels that coverage undrawn. This correction was checked against the actual close-up and rendered page.

### Required before animation production approval

1. **Finish actual coverage.** Eighteen drawn keyframes do not illustrate thirty-six distinct moving shots. K01 is the hand detail, not the separate face close-up; K14 is a tight facial reaction, not a drawn empty-roof insert. Label these accurately and draw the missing coverage before calling the board complete.
2. **Preserve the corrected K14 coverage distinction.** The delivered close-up has only a sliver of roof in the background. Its corrected tight-reaction label is accurate; the separate empty-roof insert remains a valid planned shot that must be drawn before production lock.
3. **Lock geography through movement.** The static diagram establishes a coherent intention. It does not demonstrate every camera crossing, entry, sleeve grip, retreat or path home. Stage those in the animatic, keeping all three boys accounted for. The present retreat keyframe supports the intended gate-right movement.
4. **Resolve the age and source placement with Zino.** Age fourteen / approximately 2004 is a constructed working placement within the reported secondary-school years, not a confirmed event date. The ten-year-old prologue remains visibly distinct. Do not publish the composite as a dated reconstruction.
5. **Prove performance and runtime.** Record the voices, time the long crowd passages, assemble the actual shots and revise any empty holds. Five minutes is the editorial target; there is no measured performed runtime yet.
6. **Make the next episode answer this ending.** The standalone pilot advances witness material that the sixty-episode season develops later. The next released short needs a deliberate continuation/return structure; it cannot inherit the rejected fee-notice cliffhanger or reset trauma without acknowledgement.
