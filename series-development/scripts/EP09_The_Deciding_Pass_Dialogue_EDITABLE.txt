GRAND-CHUKUCHUKU - EPISODE 09 - "THE DECIDING PASS"
SOURCE: Unified 60-Episode Plan v3 (series-development/scripts/Grand-Chukuchuku_Unified_60_Episode_Plan_v3.md), Episode 9. Scene material from the archived Full Shooting Script v2, Ep 05 (SSv2 5).
AUTHOR NOTE (20 Nov): Zino is already healed. It is a normal day, and he is eager to "chop" another cote.
STATUS: DRAFT for author edit. Pidgin lines to be recorded by the author; his recording wins where it differs.

SETTING: the Fiango road outside the compound, the bottle pitch and Grand Nestor's kiosk.
TIME AND LIGHT: late afternoon, a few days after Episode 8. Low golden sun to start, falling to dusk by the end. The streetlight is dead (SONEL), which is why the game must finish before dark.
OPENING (aerial rule): a 3 s aerial over the Fiango road and the compound roofs at golden hour, landing on the jeopardy image (Scene 1).

WARDROBE (no-long-pants rule):
- Football players wear shorts and singlets or tees, barefoot or in worn canvas shoes. Zino: white ribbed tank, royal-blue shorts.
- Gideon sits on a crate at the side and does NOT play (green polo, brown trousers).
- Luc walks the verge with his small Bible and does NOT play (mustard tee).
- Grand Nestor: red cap backwards, red plaid shirt over a white tee.

SIDES (as in Episodes 6-7): Zino, Ayuk, Robi vs Bismark (captain), Noge, Arey.
TARGETS: "33 Export" bottles (Flow assets); slippers as goalposts.
RULE: three hits on the other side's bottle wins the cote.

CONTINUITY: no crutch and no bandage. A faint pale scar on Zino's right knee. The crutch from Episode 8 leans against the compound wall.
RUNTIME TARGET: about 1:55, within the 2:00 rule.

SCENE 1 - COTE DAY (0:00-0:14)
Aerial lands on the compound gate. The crutch leans against the wall. Zino steps out past it without it, flips a coin and catches it.
ZINO: Leg don correct. Today na cote day.

SCENE 2 - THE COTE (0:14-0:36)
Grand Nestor's kiosk. The players drop coins into his upturned cap. Gideon is on his crate; Luc is on the verge.
GRAND NESTOR: Cote don full. Three hit, e done. Who hit three first, chop am.
AREY: Crutch boy don come back? Today you go waka home with empty pocket.
ZINO: Na me go chop this cote. Write am for your head.
SOUND: Gideon's laugh; a bike man rings his bell as he passes.

SCENE 3 - TWO ROUNDS (0:36-0:56)
Quick montage of the game: Ayuk hits, Arey's side answers. Two hits each. The sun drops behind the roofs and the dead streetlight stays dark.
GIDEON: Zino, sun don go house. Una go play reach morning?

SCENE 4 - THE WRONG HERO (0:56-1:14)  [THE TURN]
Ayuk is open at the better angle. Zino wants the glory and goes for the same feint as in Episode 7. Arey reads it, blocks the angle, and Zino's shot misses the bottle and rolls into the gutter. Arey's side erupts.
AYUK: Pass am!
AREY: I don know your leg. Na the same trick every time.
SOUND: laughter from Arey's side (laughter SFX).

SCENE 5 - ONE MORE CHANCE (1:14-1:34)
The ball comes back. Zino is ready to argue. Luc steps in from the verge and catches his wrist before it becomes a quarrel; he does not play.
LUC: Play the road, Zino. No play the people.
Zino looks at Ayuk, open again. He passes. Ayuk hits the third bottle. It rings and falls.
SOUND: the bottle's ring, then the crowd.

SCENE 6 - THE COST (1:34-1:48)
Grand Nestor splits the cote between Zino and Ayuk. The boys lift Ayuk, not Zino. Zino smiles, and it costs him.
GIDEON: So your leg fit hear somebody else name.

SCENE 7 - HOOK (1:48-1:55)
Dusk. A woman's voice from inside the compound calls once, then twice:
MA: Zino!
Zino freezes with the coins in his fist and quickly pushes them into his pocket. Hold the last sound over 1 s of black.
TOMORROW TAG (inside the 2:00): the landlord's notebook, open on a table.
END CARD: EPISODE 10 - RENT AND LIGHT
