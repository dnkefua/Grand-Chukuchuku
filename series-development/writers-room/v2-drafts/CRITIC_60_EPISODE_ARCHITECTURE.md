# GRAND-CHUKUCHUKU — Fiango: 60-Episode Critic Architecture v2

**Mandate:** Replace the compressed 12-episode Chapter One plan with a minimum 60-episode short-form season. Each episode runs **1:35–2:00 maximum**, in vertical 9:16. The purpose is not to add filler. It is to make Fiango feel lived in before the large milestones ask the audience to grieve, celebrate, or fear with Zino.

**Critical finding:** The former structure moved too quickly from rent pressure to fees, violence, examination and boarding. It stated a childhood but did not let viewers occupy one. The 60-episode version must earn the wider life: football at dusk, the well in the morning, compound rent silence, a saved coin, a camera booking, a bad opening line, a maths problem, a sermon, a school corridor, and the long wait around a radio.

## Format rule

Each episode needs one visible want, one turn, and one exit question. Use this short-form rhythm unless a scene needs silence:

| Time | Function |
| --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:12 | Image-led cold open: ball, well bucket, exercise book, camera strap, Bible, school bell, or fence. |
| 0:12–0:55 | Zino, Gideon, Luc, Mother, or a classmate wants something concrete. |
| 0:55–1:35 | The attempt costs status, time, money, trust, food, or control. |
| 1:35–2:00 | A changed image or question hands the audience to the next episode. |

Do not make a fantasy, a proverb, a lesson, or a cliffhanger mandatory in every episode. The daily-life episodes need different comic and emotional rhythms. A good two-minute episode can end on a bottle still standing, a girl walking away, a coin returned, a mother erasing a line, or a dormitory bell.

| Act | Episodes | Story movement | Everyday engine |

| V. **Five Papers, Another Gate** | 49–60 | Boarding becomes ordinary enough to hurt; fees, discipline and the fence test Zino before the A-Level result makes America feel near, then moved again. | Study lamp, washbasin, food line, fee pressure, punishment witness, five subjects, radio anticipation, long road home. |

## Milestone hierarchy

The audience should not reach a major milestone before the season has made its loss or gain personal.

1. **Episodes 01–12:** Build the compound, Zino's house promise, the father as inherited absence, and the three-boy chemistry. Do not bring in Professional School, jungle justice or results as a headline event.
2. **Episodes 13–24:** Let street money and school social life expose Zino's people-pleasing, Gideon's ambition and Luc's faith. Nacking Bottle should recur with a different moral or financial consequence, not repeat the same match.
3. **Episodes 25–36:** Give Professional School a full run of ordinary night activity before the crowd. The hard-R composite witness incident belongs late in this act. Its aftermath must take at least two episodes; it cannot be a spectacle followed by an exam montage.
4. **Episodes 37–48:** O-Level results, the uncle's promise, Zino's boarding choice, travel, and arrival each require their own episode. Radio success is release; it must not become an invented rank or grade.
5. **Episodes 49–60:** The fence, high-school fee exclusion, and A-Level path must remain separate pressures. The finale does not call the uncle a villain. It ends on a moved condition: first degree, then America.

## Character engines that must recur

- **Zino:** witty, gifted, just, temper-prone and eager to please. He should sometimes win the room, sometimes speak over Gideon, sometimes save a coin instead of eating, and sometimes choose restraint. He is not wise in every episode.
- **Mother:** a working primary-school teacher, with standards and agency. Use her pupils' books, her choices around the household, her fatigue, humour and boundaries. She never exists only to cry or praise Zino.
- **Gideon:** business-minded, camera-focused, often the one who pays a small social cost, and quieter with girls. Birthday-party work must be a possible camera gig, not an invented confirmed booking. His confidence grows through action, not a speech from Zino.
- **Luc:** public faith, small Bible, direct outreach, and maths anxiety. His preaching must sometimes leave him exposed. His maths work gives Zino a chance to help without becoming the hero of every scene.
- **Grand-Chukuchuku:** Zino must tell or begin an impossible story before the ideal man appears. Use him sparingly: flamboyant in Act I, tactical and increasingly questioned in Act II, silent before the crowd, nearly absent through boarding, and a diminished shadow at the uncle's deferment.

## Non-negotiable factual boundaries

Every episode and board panel must carry one or more of these tags:

- **C — Confirmed recollection:** directly supplied by Zino.
- **FT — Family testimony:** history Zino did not witness, including father's death when he was two.
- **R — Reported/incomplete:** true event, but date, order, people, wording or method is unrecorded.
- **P — Proposed composite:** dialogue, staging, invented names, exact sequence, repeated action or visual transition written for drama.

Specific safeguards:

1. **Father:** no Zino point-of-view death flashback. The snakebite account is FT; use only after a family source is recorded.
2. **Fee exclusions:** primary-school and high-school exclusions are both C, but they are separate events. Do not combine them. Keep particular teacher, amount, date and payment result P/R until known.
3. **Jungle justice:** Zino recalls at least four incidents during day-school years. One episode can be an explicitly labelled composite. The accused stays unnamed and unheard over the crowd; do not establish guilt, motive, theft, outcome, police response or body reveal. Show the supplied harm pattern through witness perspective, then show psychological consequence.
4. **Boarding fence:** Zino witnessed students punished for leaving through the fence to buy food. He did not leave. Do not invent the exact food, authority, punishment method or a hazing assault.
5. **GCE:** O Level is **10 subjects of a maximum 11**. A Level is **5 subjects of a maximum 5**. Results are read on radio. O-Level wait contains uncertainty; A-Level school pass rate is 100%, so the later wait is recognition and release, not fear that Zino will fail.
6. **Uncle:** occasional support is confirmed. The O-Level school/America promise, Zino's boarding choice, and post-A-Level first-degree deferral happen in that order. Exact words and cause of the deferral remain P/R. Never make him the later business betrayer.

## Christmas-holiday placement decision

The supplied Christmas memory—Gideon paying for drinks, Zino helping him approach girls, and a night-club night—cannot be placed inside the current 1999–2007 school chapter without an exact **18+ age/year**. The Chapter One season ends while Zino is still in school. Hold it as an **18+ Memory Card** for Chapter Two or a post-secondary bridge after the author confirms when it occurred. It may become a vivid adult contrast to Fiango football and camera hustles, but it must not be moved backward to make underage characters drink or attend a nightclub.

## Critic checklist before a v2 master is approved

1. Every episode is 2:00 or shorter on the timed read; no title card or recap hides excess dialogue.
2. Every episode has a distinct ordinary-life activity or consequence. Football, study and fantasy never repeat as interchangeable filler.
3. The 1999 primary fee exclusion and later high-school fee exclusion remain separate.
4. O Level remains 10 of 11; A Level remains 5 of 5; radio-result emotions remain distinct.
5. Gideon has at least six decisions that serve his own camera/business arc; Luc has at least six that test faith or maths; Mother makes consequential choices in every act.
6. Nacking Bottle explains the three-hit rule visually and carries no school-age alcohol depiction.
7. Professional School is visibly vocational in daylight and socially mixed at night.
8. The crowd's accused is never shown as guilty, named, explained or made into entertainment. The next two episodes carry its damage.
9. The boarding fence and fee exclusion use only supplied facts; unrecorded punishment or expulsion methods are not filled with generic cruelty.
10. The Christmas nightclub/drink story remains off this season's shooting schedule until the author supplies an 18+ year and placement.
11. Every visual brief starts from `series-development/visual-reference/VISUAL_BASELINE_V1.md` and its two author-supplied PNGs.
12. The final episode leaves a concrete Chapter Two question: can Zino turn a deferred America promise into a funded first degree?
