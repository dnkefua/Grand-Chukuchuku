# Chapter 5: Rebuilding

**Period:** 2019-2020 onward; earlier family losses placed at their actual dates

**Focus:** Grief, return, Dubai, and the difficult question of whether financial success can bring Zino peace.

**Dramatic question:** Can Zino build a life he values without allowing betrayal, grief, or the need to prove himself to define it?

**Beginning:** He is back in Cameroon after deportation, with a disrupted future and roughly six months before Dubai.

**Ending:** He becomes a successful crypto merchant, while happiness and his mother's house remain questions to develop honestly.

## Act I - Setup

Returning to Cameroon makes Zino face the distance between the future he expected and the one now available. He spends roughly six months there. Across the wider family story, the Ambazonian crisis has brought bereavement, housing loss, and disrupted livelihoods: relatives who farmed around Kumba relocate, and family members crowd into a two-bedroom home in Douala. His maternal aunt, who fed and encouraged him, is killed. Place these events at their actual dates; their aftermath can return here without implying they all happened after deportation. Home offers connection without erasing loss.

**Turning point:** Returning cannot restore the earlier world; a new path still has to be made.

## Act II - Confrontation

Zino moves to Dubai in 2020 and works for a Chinese online retailer of digital products. COVID becomes part of this new beginning. The chapter should follow the practical realities he remembers: work, money, accommodation, relationships, and the route from employment toward crypto trading. Success cannot be skipped over as a montage of wealth; specific decisions, setbacks, and learning must explain it. His technical confidence survives America, but trust and self-awareness remain dramatic questions. The same drive that helps him rebuild can also make rest, vulnerability, and happiness difficult.

**Turning point:** A new city offers a livelihood; building a meaningful life requires more than starting to earn again.

## Act III - Consequence

Zino eventually becomes a successful crypto merchant. The resolution should rest on a true decision, relationship, or change he can describe, rather than an invented purchase or complete emotional recovery. His mother's house has not been built. That open goal gives success a human scale and prevents the ending from pretending every debt of love has been settled. The proposed redemption is a shift toward a more honest definition of strength: accepting limits, recognizing help, and deciding what to do with opportunity. Grand-Chukuchuku can remain part of his voice without being the standard he must meet.

**Turning point:** The story ends with a future he can act on, while the promise to his mother remains open.
