# GRAND-CHUKUCHUKU — Fiango, 60-Episode Season

## Writer C draft — Episodes 25–36

**Block placement:** Later Fiango day-school / O-Level preparation, approximately 2004–2005. Every episode is built for 9:16 animation, English and Cameroon Pidgin with subtitles, and a maximum running time of two minutes.

**Block movement:** The boys turn a school that teaches trades by day into a shared night-world of revision, freedom and avoidance. Zino learns that preparation is not the same as control. A primary-school fee memory returns when money pressure makes concentration difficult; a composite jungle-justice witness event then changes the emotional meaning of the night spaces. The block ends at the beginning of the ten-paper O-Level push, before the results or boarding-school decision.

**Fact key:** **C** confirmed recollection supplied by Zino; **FT** family testimony; **R** reported/incomplete detail; **P** proposed composite or dialogue. All dialogue below is P unless explicitly stated otherwise.
