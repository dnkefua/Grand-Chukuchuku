## 2. Detailed Critique by Category

### A. Character & Costume Continuity (Critical Fixes Needed)

1. **Major Costume & Character Model Break in Shots `C19` & `C20` (`01:54–02:02`):**
   - Across `A01`–`C15` and again in `C22`, **Zino** wears a **white sleeveless singlet, blue shorts, black low-top canvas shoes**, and has a **tight curly fade**.
   - In **`C19`** (*"Give me one minute I d cam"*) and **`C20`** (sprinting into the compound courtyard), Zino suddenly transforms into a different character design: **faded red/orange sleeveless vest over a grey undershirt, khaki/olive shorts, and taller spiky hair** (inherited from an older pre-rebuild keyframe in the Flow library).
   - Then in **`C22`** (*"Ma! Landlord dee come!"*), he snaps back to his **white singlet and blue shorts**.
2. **Gideon's Duplicate Back-to-Back Shots (`B09` v1 + `B09` v2):**
   - On the timeline around `00:54–01:18`, there are **two consecutive 8-second shots of Gideon in his green polo** (`Gideon speaking to friends` followed by `Character speaking about French …`). Keeping only the stronger take (`Character speaking about French …`) will immediately tighten Act II by 8 seconds and eliminate the jarring jump cut from Gideon to Gideon on the exact same camera angle.
3. **Foreground Listener Shirts in Over-the-Shoulder (OTS) Shots:**
   - In `A02`, Zino is framed OTS between Gideon (green left) and Luc (yellow right).
   - In `B07` and `B12A`, the right-foreground OTS boy wears a **dark grey shirt** instead of Luc's yellow shirt, then flips back to yellow in `B12C`.

### B. Background & Spatial Geography Critique

1. **The "Ghost Footballers" Continuity Bug (`A02`–`C14`):**
   - In `A01`, the football match stops when Zino steps over to talk to Gideon and Luc, and in `A06` all the boys stop and hold the ball while the motorcycle passes.
   - However, in the background of `A02`, `A03`, `A04`, `B09`, `B11A`, `B12A`, `B12B`, `B12C`, and `C14`, **miniature clones of the boys are still actively kicking a football in the middle of the road** behind the speaking characters, and in `B12A`/`B12C` a stationary cyclist is frozen mid-road beside them.
2. **`PROFESSIONAL SCHOOL` Sign Placement & 180-Degree Axis Jumps:**
   - **Canonical Layout (`A01`, `B09`, `B11A`):** Looking down the unpaved brown road, the **unlabeled residential compound wall/gate is on the LEFT**, and the **`PROFESSIONAL SCHOOL` gate/archway is on the RIGHT**.
   - **Shot `A03` (`00:13–00:23`):** Behind Gideon's right shoulder on the **LEFT** side of the road, a partial sign reading `PROFESSI...` appears on what should be the residential compound side.
   - **Shot `B07` (`00:43–00:50`):** The `PROFESSIONAL SCHOOL` gate is suddenly **perpendicular at the far end of a T-junction** behind Zino instead of running parallel along the right side of the road.
3. **Compound Courtyard Architecture (`B13`, `C16`–`C18`, `C20`, `C22`, `C23`):**
   - **Strengths:** The compound interior shots (`B13`, `C16`, `C17`, `C18`, `C22`, `C23`) share a convincing, cohesive spatial grammar: ochre/cream plastered walls, unpainted wooden shutters/doors, packed-earth courtyard, the central **mango tree**, and the **circular stone well**.
   - **Inconsistency in `C20`:** When Zino sprints through the courtyard in `C20`, the compound suddenly looks like a sprawling **open school quadrangle with a manicured green lawn** behind the mango tree and no stone well, before returning to the tighter earth courtyard with the stone well in `C22` and `C23`.
4. **Shot `C23` Ending vs. Script Rule ("Unresolved Before Knock"):**
   - The production plan states: *"Final shot holds on the landlord arriving at the compound gate — unresolved, before any knock or confrontation."*
   - In the second half of `C23`, the video cuts to an insert of the landlord's hand **already knocking on a wooden door**. Trimming the last 1.5 seconds of `C23` so it holds on him stepping through the open metal gate honors the cliffhanger rule.

### C. Sound Design, Voice Acting & Audio Engineering Critique

1. **Loudness & Dynamic Range (`EBU R128` Analysis):**
   - **Integrated Loudness:** `-14.4 LUFS` (ideal for web/streaming).
   - **Loudness Range (`LRA`):** `15.3 LU` (`LRA low: -29.5 LUFS` to `LRA high: -14.2 LUFS`). Dialogue shots punch at `-14 LUFS`, while atmospheric/action shots (`B13`, `C20`, `C21`, `C23`) drop nearly `-15 dB` quieter.
2. **Hard Audio Dropouts & Silence Gaps (`silencedetect` Findings):**
   - **`01:31.55 – 01:35.37` (`3.82 seconds` of near-total silence during Shot `B13`):** Audio drops below `-35 dB` for almost the entire 4-second window-peeking clip.
   - **`00:59.27 – 01:00.58` (`1.31s` gap), `01:17.93 – 01:19.02` (`1.09s` gap), `02:15.89 – 02:17.31` (`1.42s` gap):** Every clip splice resets the ambient room tone because each clip was generated independently.
3. **Voice Timbre Consistency (`C16` vs `C17`):**
   - While the custom Kumba Pidgin voices (`Zino`, `Gideon`, `Luc`) remain consistent across Acts I–III, Tenant W1's native-generated voice shifts timbre between `C16` and `C17`.
