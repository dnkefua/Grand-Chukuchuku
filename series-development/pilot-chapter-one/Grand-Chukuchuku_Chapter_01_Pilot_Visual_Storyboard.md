# GRAND-CHUKUCHUKU
## “The Courtyard Keeps Score” — 5-minute visual storyboard

**Screen:** 9:16 vertical  
**Panel count:** 17 timed boards  
**Status:** Production storyboard for the proposed Chapter One pilot. It is derived from confirmed setting and character facts, with proposed scene action clearly marked in the accompanying shooting script.

## Visual premise

The camera treats Fiango as a lived-in arena. The home compound is both a playground and a ledger: the mango tree and well hold laughter, work, debt and shame in the same frame. Grand-Chukuchuku changes only Zino’s imagination—navy, gold and impossibly clean motion—not the physical world.

## Colour and camera rules

- **Real Fiango:** rust-red earth, sun-faded plaster, mango green, laundry colour, warm afternoon gold, later deep teal shadow.
- **Fantasy:** the same geography, opened by a precise navy-and-gold light trail. Grand-Chukuchuku remains fully human and poised; he never becomes a superhero.
- **Faces:** keep Zino low in the frame against a large compound or sky whenever pressure is larger than he is. Shift to close-up only when he chooses a story or realises the cost of a choice.
- **Subtitles:** white, high-contrast, sentence case; place above the lower safe area and never cover eyes, the well rope or the coins.

## Required production assets

1. **Zino, Gideon and Luc child turnarounds:** 3/4, profile, back and four key expressions each. Preserve the family resemblance between Zino and adult Grand; keep Gideon’s compact camera and Luc’s small Bible distinct.
2. **Mother turnaround:** teacher work clothes, home apron layer, exercise-book prop, controlled concern and warm private smile.
3. **Compound master layout:** mango tree, well, six-to-eight visible household fronts, laundry runs, road entrance, footpath to school. The user-supplied compound reference is the authority.
4. **Street-football kit:** scuffed ball, slippers, folded cloth, period-neutral coins, two bottle targets. Bottles are sporting markers only.
5. **Fantasy package:** adult Grand’s canonical navy suit, gold light curve, roofline silhouette, particles. It must switch off instantly when the story ends.
6. **Sound library:** well rope, bucket, compound radio, street football, classroom chalk, evening insects and one short signature Grand-Chukuchuku gold-chime motif.

## Animation priorities

- Spend animation detail on Zino’s first shot, the bottle hit, Mother’s response to the notice, and the hand opening with coins.
- Make the fantasy an elegant compositing event, not a separate action sequence. Its purpose is to reveal Zino’s mind.
- Let the landlord sequence play in the background. The child does not understand every adult word; the audience understands the pressure from the silence.
- Keep the final school-fee beat specific in emotion and non-specific in historical detail until Zino confirms the original context.
