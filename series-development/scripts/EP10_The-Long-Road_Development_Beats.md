# GRAND-CHUKUCHUKU

## Episode 10 — “The Long Road”

**Target length:** 2 minutes 30 seconds
**Format:** Vertical 9:16 animated short
**Story period:** Beginning of one of Zino's final two boarding high-school years, around 2005
**Draft status:** Development beats — confirmed setting with proposed scene arrangement

### Purpose

After five years of day school in Fiango with Luc and Gideon, Zino enters a remote boarding high school. It takes a day to reach the village, through mountains and valleys. The school brings a drastic change in distance, routine, discipline, social class, teachers, priests, bullying, and the meaning of home.

The travel method, school name, exact year, specific teachers, dormitory rules, and individual bullying events still need Zino's memories. This episode should use the confirmed emotional truth without inventing details.

---

### Act I — Leaving the compound (0:00–0:42)

The compound begins crowded and familiar: the well, mango tree, a ball in the dirt, and neighbors who know Zino's stories. He packs for a school that feels too far away to be real. His mother checks what he has with the practical eye of a primary-school teacher. Zino performs confidence for her and for himself. Grand-Chukuchuku appears in fantasy, crossing a mountain in one stride with a suitcase on his shoulder.

**Immediate question:** Can Zino carry Fiango's confidence into a place built for children who seem more prepared than he is?

### Act II — The road and the gate (0:42–1:45)

The journey lasts a day. Mountains and valleys replace the cramped streets of Fiango. As the school comes into view, Zino notices polished luggage, parents with money, and students arriving with the confidence of people who know the rules. White teachers and priests move through the gate with authority. The school is beautiful and imposing, but Zino sees immediately that beauty can also be a warning: he is far from the compound, his mother, Luc, and Gideon.

**Turn:** Zino has reached the school he is supposed to want, and it makes him feel small.

### Act III — First night (1:45–2:30)

The dormitory imposes routine: lines, bed spaces, lights, food, uniforms, rules, and older students who understand how power works. A bully tests a new student's weakness. Zino's temper rises, but he must decide whether to react or learn the order of the place first. The bell cuts through the dark. Grand-Chukuchuku does not appear. Zino lies awake, listening to voices he does not yet trust.

**End button:** In the morning, Zino sees the fence that other students will later risk to cross for food.
