# GRAND-CHUKUCHUKU — STORYBOARD PRODUCTION GUIDE
## EPISODE 04 / 05: "KNACKING BOTTLE" (STREET FOOTBALL & MARTIAL ARTS BRAWL)

**Format:** 2D Expressive Adult Animation, Vertical 9:16 (with 16:9 cinematic safety grid)  
**Location:** Fiango, Kumba (Cameroon) — Red dirt street, sunbaked earth, sun-bleached zinc roofs, mango trees  
**Production Target:** Ready-to-paste Prompts for Google Flow / Imagen Image Generation to maximize daily credits  
**Series Canon & Rules:**
1. **Knacking Bottle Rules:** Two glass beer bottles (e.g. "33" Export or Guinness) standing ~15m apart on the bare dirt road. Touching the bottle with the ball counts as a goal/hit (First to 3 hits wins). No offside. Players can play 360 degrees around and behind the bottle.
2. **Cote / Bet (The Stakes):** 6 players (3 vs 3). Each player stakes 100 CFA francs = 600 CFA pot placed in a tin cup / folded cloth at the sideline. Winner takes all (200 CFA per winning player).
3. **Sideline Roles:** Gideon does NOT play; he sits on a crate/verandah bantering, laughing, and observing. Luc does NOT play; he evangelizes or steps in trying to preach peace.
4. **Action Aesthetic:** Hyper-kinetic, gritty street football, rainbow flicks, elastico dribbles, brutal sliding tackles on gravel/dirt leaving bleeding scrapes, escalating into an insane martial arts / high karate street fight in rising clouds of red dust.
