# GRAND-CHUKUCHUKU

## Episode 09 — “Results on Air”

**Target length:** 2 minutes 30 seconds
**Format:** Vertical 9:16 animated short
**Story period:** End of Zino's fifth Fiango day-school year, around 2005
**Draft status:** Development beats — confirmed academic milestones with proposed scene arrangement

### Purpose

At the end of five years of day school, Zino writes ten GCE Ordinary-Level subjects out of a maximum eleven. The wait for results is a public ritual: students and families listen to names read over the radio because there are no smartphones. The joy after a name is called is communal, physical, and unforgettable. Proud of Zino's result, his uncle promises that Zino can choose any school and go to America if he succeeds in all five Advanced-Level subjects.

This episode also needs to carry the aftereffect of “The Crowd.” Zino is trying to return to study, jokes, football, and friendship after seeing something he cannot make light of.

---

### Act I — Back to the books (0:00–0:42)

Zino returns to day school after the jungle-justice event. He tries to joke with Gideon and Luc, but the joke does not land. Miss Mukete brings the room back to chemistry and the coming GCE Ordinary Levels. Zino has ten subjects to write. He wants schoolwork to give him a clean, controllable future.

**Immediate question:** Can achievement quiet what Zino has seen?

### Act II — The wait (0:42–1:42)

Examinations pass in a hard montage of exercise books, revision at Professional School, school fees, heat, fatigue, and the compound's ordinary life continuing around him. Months later, a group gathers around a radio. Before a name is read, every pause feels too long. One student celebrates; another waits. Zino watches his mother without knowing whether he is more afraid of disappointing her or seeing how much she hopes.

**Turn:** The result is not private. It belongs to every person who sacrificed, waited, studied, and believed.

### Act III — A name on air (1:42–2:30)

Zino hears his name. The reaction should come from his exact memory: laughter, crying, a run through the compound, church, food, music, or a quiet embrace. Do not invent the grade, the order of names, or who was beside the radio. The radio keeps speaking, but for one moment Zino hears his uncle's pride turn into an offer: choose the school you want; succeed in all five Advanced-Level subjects, and America will follow. Zino chooses boarding school.

**End button:** His packed books sit beside a route he has never travelled, and the word “America” now has a condition attached.
