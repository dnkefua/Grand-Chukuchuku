# Grand-Chukuchuku continuity log

This log separates confirmed additions from items that still need a date, source, or naming decision. It is the reference to update before changing a script, outline, or animation prompt.

## Added on 28 October 2026

- Zino's father died after a snake bite while spraying cocoa. In the family account, he cut the bite area and tried to suck out the venom, then passed out before reaching home and died with his right arm swollen. His death date, Zino's age, and the immediate aftermath are still open.
- Zino's mother was a primary-school teacher from a young age and throughout his childhood.
- Gideon, previously recorded as Gideo, is sharp and business-minded. He bought his first camera in secondary school, often paid when he and Zino went out, and valued Zino's help speaking with girls.
- Luc is God-fearing, carries a small Gideons Bible, preaches to students, and values Zino's honesty, humour, mathematics help, and conversations about girls.
- The Fiango compound holds two families, three single mothers, two bachelors, and three student tenants. The landlord collects monthly rent and electricity money.
- Zino reports witnessing at least four incidents of jungle justice in Fiango against people accused of theft. Scenes will center fear, aftermath, and moral consequence rather than graphic violence.
- Around June, evening study sessions called Professional School gather students for examination preparation. Some study; others socialize, smoke, drink, pursue relationships, or use the session as a reason to leave home. Do not treat this as true of every student.
