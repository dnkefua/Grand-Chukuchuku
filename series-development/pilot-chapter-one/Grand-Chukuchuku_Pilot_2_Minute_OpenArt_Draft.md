# GRAND-CHUKUCHUKU

## Chapter One — two-minute pilot draft

**Runtime:** 2:00  
**Format:** 16:9 master, with a protected central crop for 9:16 social preview  
**Visual direction:** stylised cinematic 2D animation; Fiango, late afternoon to dusk  
**Purpose:** Introduce Zino, Gideon, Luc, Grand-Chukuchuku, the compound and the school-fee pressure in one escalating sequence.

## Story compression

The original five-minute pilot's school-morning and Nacking Bottle sequences move to the next episode. This draft ends immediately after the school notice, turning Zino's fantasy of "one more chance" into the hook.

## Locked asset rules

- Use established character references for Zino, Gideon, Luc, Mother, Mirabel and Lizzy where present; never use generic replacements for named roles.
- GBHS daytime students wear the short-sleeved, three-pocket sky-blue jacket, dark-blue lower uniform and circular **GBHS Kumba** badge on the wearer's upper-left chest pocket only.
- Grand-Chukuchuku is imaginary: he appears only inside Zino's storytelling transition and cannot solve the real problem.
- The Fiango compound retains the mango tree, well, laundry, red-earth ground and close-set homes.

## OpenArt render plan

Render shots 01–11 as separate 4–16 second clips. Use image-to-video for composition-locked shots (01, 03, 05, 07, 09–11) and element-to-video for character action in new shots (02, 04, 06, 08). Generate dialogue as separate controlled audio; do not rely on model-generated speech for story-critical lines. Assemble in an editor only after visual approvals.

## First-draft acceptance checks

1. Zino, Gideon, Luc and Mother match their established reference identities in every shot.
2. Grand-Chukuchuku appears only in Shot 04.
3. No named character's dialogue is improvised or replaced.
4. The final notice lands as a quiet, clear cliffhanger rather than melodrama.
