GRAND-CHUKUCHUKU - EPISODE 08 - "THE MESSAGE"
SOURCE: Unified 60-Episode Plan v3 (series-development/scripts/Grand-Chukuchuku_Unified_60_Episode_Plan_v3.md), Episode 8 "The Message". Scene material from the archived Full Shooting Script v2, Ep 08 (SSv2 8).

STATUS: DRAFT for author edit. Pidgin lines to be recorded by the author.
SETTING: GBHS Kumba day school, classroom veranda / corridor at morning break (Blender GBHS campus asset). Look: 3D cinematic.
WARDROBE: day-school uniform - light sky-blue short-sleeved shirts; dark blue trousers (boys), dark blue skirts (girls).
CHARACTERS: Zino, Gideon, Luc (locked designs), THE GIRL (new, name to be decided by author), her two friends (no lines), classmates in the background.
RUNTIME TARGET: about 1:50

SCENE 1 - THE ASK (0:00-0:16)
Corridor at break. Gideon sees the girl who was watching yesterday's bottle match, standing with two friends. He changes direction twice.
GIDEON: Talk to her for me.
ZINO: You want me talk, or you want me become you?

SCENE 2 - ZINO'S OPENING (0:16-0:45)
Zino starts a harmless conversation about yesterday's match. The girl is amused. Gideon stays behind Zino, unable to come in.
ZINO: Gideon fit commentate match like radio. Even when ball no move, e go shout "Chai!"
GIDEON: I never say that.

SCENE 3 - MAKE ROOM (0:45-1:15)
Everyone waits for Zino's next joke. He stops and gestures Gideon forward. Gideon asks one ordinary question himself.
GIDEON: You see how the third bottle fall?
THE GIRL: I see am. E ring like church bell.
SOUND: the school bell cuts in before anyone can overplay the moment.

SCENE 4 - THE COST OF CHARM (1:15-1:35)
The girl walks away with her friends. Gideon is pleased, then annoyed that Zino made it look easy.
GIDEON: Next time, no talk plenty.
ZINO: Next time, use your own mouth first.

SCENE 5 - HOOK (1:35-1:50)
Luc walks up to another student alone, Bible in hand. Zino watches, a grin forming.
END CARD: EPISODE 09 - LUC'S ONE MINUTE
