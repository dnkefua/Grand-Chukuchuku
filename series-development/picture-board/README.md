## Visual system

- **Look:** Original expressive 2D adult-animation key art. Strong silhouettes, textured colour, sharp facial acting, cinematic light, and no imitation of a living artist or a specific show.
- **Fiango palette:** red earth, mango green, sun-faded plaster, warm gold, and deep evening blue. The compound is circular and close-set, with the well and mango tree as its visual centre.
- **Camera:** Characters fill the lower two-thirds in 9:16. The top third carries sky, trees, school architecture, or approaching threat. Keep close-ups for Zino's moments of imagination or rupture.
- **Grand-Chukuchuku rule:** He is visibly heightened and separate from the real world. Use him only when Zino actively tells an impossible story. After the crowd, imagery becomes incomplete, quiet, or absent.
- **Violence rule:** The hard-R master keeps the witness view and consequences of jungle justice. The board does not identify the accused, establish guilt, show a body, or turn suffering into spectacle.
