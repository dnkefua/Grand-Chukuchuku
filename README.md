# Grand-Chukuchuku

An autobiographical animated thriller based on the life of Nkeh Nkengzincho, known as Zino: Fiango, education in America, deportation, and rebuilding in Dubai.

## In the same school uniform

[Mirabel uniform PNG](series-development/character-bible/turnarounds/school-uniforms/04_Mirabel_Uniform_Turnaround.png) · [Lizzy uniform PNG](series-development/character-bible/turnarounds/school-uniforms/05_Lizzy_Uniform_Turnaround.png)

[Uniform comparison PDF](series-development/character-bible/turnarounds/school-uniforms/Grand-Chukuchuku_Uniform_Comparison.pdf) · [Download uniform images and PDFs](series-development/Grand-Chukuchuku_School_Uniform_Characters_v1.zip)

Both wear the same sky-blue shirt and dark-blue skirt. Mirabel is darker than Lizzy and retains her slim silhouette/angular oval face; Lizzy retains her rounder face and existing natural proportions. The requested body-size edit was declined by the image tool; it was not applied. Clothing-only uniform models were completed.

**Wardrobe correction:** the sky-blue/navy uniform belongs to daytime school. At Professional School night revision, every student wears ordinary house/casual clothes; some dress to impress. The current pilot art uses consistent casual clothes through study, the road and the return home.
