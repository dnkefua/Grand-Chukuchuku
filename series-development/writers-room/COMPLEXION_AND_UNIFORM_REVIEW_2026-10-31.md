## Requirements that govern this revision

- Both characters are Black Cameroonian girls with **low-cut natural hair**. No bob, puffs, braids, ribbons or invented accessories may be used to distinguish them.
- Mirabel's complexion is darker than Lizzy's. The contrast must be consistent over all exposed skin and all three views, rather than an isolated darker face or a change of lighting.
- Mirabel retains a slim silhouette and proposed longer, more angular oval face with straighter brows. Lizzy retains her existing rounder face, more arched brows and current natural overall proportions. The school-age drawings remain fully clothed and neutral, without emphasis on intimate anatomy.
- Both daytime uniforms use the confirmed **light sky-blue short-sleeved shirt and dark-blue skirt**. Knee-length rendering, plain dark flat shoes and shirt fit are model proposals. Different uniform colours, trousers, badges, ties or added jewellery would not satisfy the requested distinction.
- Each uniform character must match her own casual model's face, complexion and low crop. Front, profile and back should keep a consistent outfit, identity, scale and baseline.
- Daytime uniform does not change the separate Professional School night-revision rule: students wear house/casual clothes at night.
