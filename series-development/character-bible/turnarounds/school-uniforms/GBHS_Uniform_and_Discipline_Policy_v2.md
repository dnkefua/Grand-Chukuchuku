# GBHS uniform and badge discipline — v2

**Status:** Author-supplied character and production canon. This sheet supersedes conflicting shirt-only depictions in older uniform references.

## Daytime jacket

- The upper uniform is a **short-sleeved school jacket**, not a plain shirt-only costume. Long sleeves are incorrect for this uniform.
- It has **three pockets**: two lower-front pockets and one upper-left chest pocket (wearer's left; viewer-right in a frontal image).
- The GBHS badge is fixed to the upper-left chest pocket. It must never be mirrored onto the right chest, duplicated, or placed on the back.
- Existing daytime uniform colours remain the established light sky-blue upper garment with dark-blue trousers for boys and dark-blue skirts for girls unless a later author instruction changes the palette.

## Story rule: badge inspection

- A student without their badge may get into trouble at school.
- The Discipline Master sometimes walks through the campus to inspect badges.
- During an inspection, the Discipline Master takes the names of students without badges for work punishment.
- This is a recurring school-life rule, not a claim about any named student's punishment in a particular episode. Use it only when the scene calls for it.

## Image and animation checks

1. Show the badge only on the jacket's upper-left pocket.
2. Preserve all three pockets in front/three-quarter views when visible; a side or back view may naturally conceal them.
3. For badge-check scenes, use readable staging: the Discipline Master approaches or observes students; do not turn the check into public violence or humiliation.
4. Professional School night scenes remain casual clothes, with no GBHS jacket or badge.
