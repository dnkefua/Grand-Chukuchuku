GRAND CHUKUCHUKU
Two-minute pilot dialogue - editable draft

The boys have finished primary school and are on holiday before the new secondary-school year. The school names for Luc and Gideon are placeholders for your correction. Timing is a guide; the final length depends on the performances and pauses.

ACT ONE: MEETING ON THE ROAD (0:00-0:35)

[On the road between the established compound and Professional School, Zino taps a football as he meets Gideon and Luc. At least three other children are actively playing nearby. Gideon and Luc watch rather than play. Tenants are visible around the compound, and pedestrians, bicycles and motorbikes use the road.]

ZINO: Giddy! Giddy. Wah Pastor Lucky Luc! Blood deh my eye. Which school wuna d go? Me I do GBHS up station.

GIDEON: Zino, you and this ball! Why you nor go Kadji Sport for play football? Me to na GB I d go. How for you Luc?

LUC: Same. Doe nor deh like I for like for go SASSE.

ZINO: SASSE weh ngah them nor deh? Never! Even if I want go bording school I must go na mix. Massa ma nack small ndamba.

PLAYER: Bike! Make we wait!

[The players stop. They wait for the motorbike and pedestrians to pass, then resume.]

ACT TWO: THE NEXT CHAPTER (0:35-1:20)

ZINO: Man go start sap like professional. Jacket and long trousers. Norbi that short nicka for primary school.

LUC: Population and distance for waka. Chai under hot sun.

GIDEON: Zino I hear say fine fine French girls them deh that school. Just make sure say ya man get one I beg. 

ZINO: Nor worry Giddi make we first reach Ju Je the atmosphere. 

GIDEON: Secondary school boys now!

LUC: New school, new teachers... everything new.

ZINO: Wuna think say secondary school go hard?

GIDEON: If e hard, you go explain am for we!

ZINO: For free?

[They laugh. The street game and ordinary neighbourhood life continue behind them.]

ACT THREE: THE LANDLORD (1:20-2:00)

[Zino notices the landlord coming toward the compound. His excitement stops. Tenants in the background begin closing doors and windows; a few try to leave. The mood tightens, but the street and compound remain inhabited.]

ZINO: Wait... na landlord.

GIDEON: E dey come for compound?

ZINO: Give me one minute I d cam.

[Zino runs toward his mother's room. His friends watch from the road. The landlord continues approaching; he has not yet confronted anyone.]

ZINO: Ma! Landlord dee come!

[CUT TO BLACK before Mother's response. What happens when the landlord arrives carries into the next episode.]

PRODUCTION NOTES

- Keep the compound exactly consistent with the established layout opposite Professional School: the same relationship between the road, school, compound rooms, mango tree and well.
- Show tenants throughout compound shots. When the landlord appears, most close doors or windows, and some try to leave.
- Keep the road active with pedestrians and bikes. Street football pauses for people and vehicles to pass.
- Whenever Zino has the ball on the street, show at least three other active players. Gideon and Luc remain spectators.
- No image, portrait, silhouette or fantasy appearance of Grand Chukuchuku in the pilot.
- Use continuous street and compound ambience, motivated sound bridges and clean cuts. Produce dialogue as separate voice tracks and align lip movement to the approved performances.
