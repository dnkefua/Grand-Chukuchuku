THE RULES OF KNACKING BOTTLE:
- Pitch: Red dirt street in Fiango, Kumba (~15m long).
- Goals: Two empty glass beer bottles ("33" Export / Guinness) stood upright at opposite ends.
- Scoring: Hitting the bottle with the ball counts as a goal/hit. First to 3 hits wins.
- Field Dynamic: No offside! Players can roam 360 degrees and even play behind the bottle to shoot or deflect.
- Cote / Bet: 6 players (3 vs 3). Each player stakes 100 CFA francs (600 CFA pot). Winning team takes the 600 CFA (200 CFA per player).
- Spectators: Gideon sits on a crate/verandah bantering; Luc evangelises/preaches peace from the sideline.

---

SCENE 1 — THE COTE ON THE DIRT
On the hot, sun-baked red dirt road, six boys put down their 100-franc coins on a folded cloth beside an upright beer bottle. 15 meters away stands the opposing bottle.

GIDEON (from his crate, laughing): You sure say your lunch money want play today? Once that glass knack, no refund oh!

ZINO (tying his worn canvas shoes): My lunch money get ambition. 100 francs enter, 200 francs come out.

AREY (opposing captain): You dey talk like say bottle no dey waka. First to three knacks. All man inside!


SCENE 2 — NO OFFSIDE / BEHIND-THE-BOTTLE AMBUSH
Play starts at blinding speed. Red dust billows. Arey sprints behind Zino's beer bottle, setting up an ambush angle.

AREY: Pass am behind! No offside for this road!

AYUK: Block am! Cover the glass!


SCENE 3 — CRAZY STREET DRIBBLE & SLIDING TACKLE
Zino cuts through midfield with an insane elastico nutmeg. As he winds up to strike at the far bottle, defender Noge throws a brutal sliding tackle across the dry gravel.
Red dust explodes. Zino's shin and knees are scraped raw, trickling blood onto the red dirt. He jumps back up without flinching.

GIDEON: Chai! Leg don shave gravel!

ZINO (wiping sweat and grit): You come play ball or you come scrape my skin?!


SCENE 4 — THE KNACK!
Zino fakes a pass to Ayuk, turns on a dime, and fires a laser strike low across the dirt.
SOUND: Crisp, metallic CLINK-CLACK as the ball clips the opposing beer bottle! The bottle spins violently and topples into the dirt!

ZINO & AYUK: ONE KNACK! ONE KNACK DON ENTER! GIMME MY MONEY!


SCENE 5 — THE DISPUTE
Arey charges forward, waving his arms, contesting the hit.

AREY: E touch stone before e touch bottle! That one no count!

ZINO: You dey blind or your ears dey block?! The bottle sing like bell! Hand over the 600 francs!

LUC (rushing from the sideline, arms open): Wuna stop! No turn 100 francs game to war! Bible say blessed are the peacemakers!

AREY: Move comot for road, Luc! Nobody call pastor!


SCENE 6 — HIGH KARATE / MARTIAL ARTS STREET BRAWL
Words explode into a full-scale martial arts fight in the swirling red dust!
Arey throws a wild haymaker; Zino ducks underneath and unleashes a breathtaking flying karate dropkick in mid-air.
Boys clash with sweeping kicks, jumping blocks, and spinning martial arts dodges across the road.

GIDEON (jumping on his wooden crate, roaring with laughter): Yee-haa! Fiango Shaolin! Bruce Lee don enter Kumba!

In the scuffle, someone kicks the beer bottle against a roadside rock—CRASH!
From the compound gate, Mother's voice thunders across the street:
MOTHER (O.S.): ZINO! WUNA DEY CRAZE FOR THAT ROAD?!

In a second, the fight scatters into the wind. Boys bolt in every direction, leaving the ball and the coins glinting in the twilight dust.
