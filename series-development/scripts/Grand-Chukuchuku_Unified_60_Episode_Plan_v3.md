# Grand Chuckuchucku: Unified 60-Episode Plan, Version 3 (CURRENT)

**Status:** the plan in use from 20 Nov 2026. The author adopted it after answering its six open decisions (section 6).
- It supersedes Unified v2, which is archived in `series-development/archive/unified-plan_v2_ARCHIVED/`.
- **Built from:** the author's "Recommendations" note of 20 Nov (target 8.5), raised to a 9.5 target, and the canon carried over from Unified v2.
- **Target:** every episode **9.5 or above** on the rubric below. These are design targets: each episode is measured at its script gate, and anything under 9.5 is rewritten before it is boarded.
- **Labels:** "SSv2 N" means episode N of the archived Full Shooting Script v2 (`series-development/archive/shooting-script_v1_v2_ARCHIVED/`). It is scene material only; never take its episode order.

**Raised beyond 8.5 to 9.5:**
1. **Six season-long mysteries** run through all 60 episodes (section 3), so every episode moves at least one open question.
2. **Mystery, not irony (author, 20 Nov).** The girl's secret is kept from the audience as well as from Zino.
   - Before the reveal there are only clues that make sense in hindsight: she won't be photographed and runs at a whistle (Ep 11), and the negative of her photo disappears (Ep 13).
   - In **Ep 19**, Zino and the audience learn she is the discipline master's daughter **at the same moment**.
3. **Two antagonist ladders** escalate across the season instead of resetting every episode:
   - **Arey** at the bottles and on the street;
   - **the big boy** at school: he tore the page in Ep 4, and in Ep 21 he gives it back with Zino's name added.
4. **A cost rule:** every episode takes something from Zino that does not come back the next day (money, trust, an object, a friendship, face).
5. **Mirror crowds:** in Ep 16 the crowd spares Grand K; in Eps 33–35 a crowd does not spare. Act III's horror lands because Act II showed mercy.

## 2. Thrill 9.5 rubric (checked at the script gate)

| # | Check | Points |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Cold open:** the aerial (always first when an episode opens at home or school) runs 3 seconds at most and lands on, or cuts straight to, an image of jeopardy. Never a plain location card. | 1.0 |
| 2 | **Stake on screen:** something Zino can visibly lose (coin, camera, seat, badge, Ma's trust, a friend). | 1.5 |
| 3 | **Named opposition:** a person, an institution or a clock with a face (Arey, the big boy, the DM, the landlord, the bell, the fee notice). | 1.0 |
| 4 | **A visible clock** that forces a choice in the last 30 seconds. | 1.5 |
| 5 | **A turn between 0:55 and 1:10:** the plan fails, a helper becomes the problem, or the truth flips. | 1.5 |
| 6 | **A cost** that carries into the next episode. | 1.0 |
| 7 | **A season loop touched:** a mystery advanced, never only mentioned. | 1.0 |
| 8 | **A hook in the last 10 seconds** that the next episode's first 15 seconds answer or worsen. | 1.0 |

- **9.5** means all eight checks are met, with at most 0.5 lost on execution.
- **Penalty:** an engine already used in an earlier episode costs 1.0 unless the stakes are higher **and** the antagonist has learned something since.

## 5. Edit rules from Episode 8 upwards (author, 20 Nov)
- **Episodes 1–7 stay exactly as finished.** No edit-only lifts.
- **From Episode 8 up, on both the YouTube and TikTok cuts:**
  - the aerial runs 3 s at most and lands on the jeopardy image (rubric check 1);
  - the final line or sound is held over 1 s of black before the end card;
  - a **"tomorrow" tag** of up to 3 s sits inside the 2:00: one unexplained image from the next episode's first locked board frame. So each episode's successor must be boarded before the episode is packaged.
- **Episode 8:**
  - aerial A01 trimmed from 5 s to 3 s;
  - a 1.5 s flash of the big boy watching (from B11);
  - hold over black;
  - tag from Ep 9's first board frame;
  - trim D01 slightly so the total stays within 2:00.
