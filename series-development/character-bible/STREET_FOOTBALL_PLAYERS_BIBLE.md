# GRAND-CHUKUCHUKU — STREET FOOTBALL PLAYERS CHARACTER BIBLE
## Fiango Street Football Circle & "Knacking Bottle" Squads

**Canon Source:** Nkeh Nkengzincho (Zino) confirmed neighborhood peers and classmates in Fiango, Kumba.  
**Mandatory Wardrobe Rule:**
> **STRICT NO-LONG-PANTS RULE FOR FOOTBALL PLAYERS:**  
> In street football in Fiango, **NO PLAYER WEARS LONG PANTS.**  
> Every player wears athletic **shorts** (above the knee), sleeveless singlets/tank tops or lightweight t-shirts, and plays either barefoot or in worn canvas sneakers/trainers.  
> Long pants are strictly reserved for school uniform or evening casual wear (Luc wears long casual trousers because Luc does NOT play football; Gideon sits on the sideline bantering and also does not play).

### TEAM 1: ZINO'S SIDE
*Colors / Silhouette: Green, Blue & White trims*

#### 1. ZINO (Team Captain / Main Protagonist)
* **Status:** Existing model sheet (`01_Zino_Turnaround.png`, `Zino — pilot`).
* **Physique & Face:** Handsome, clean low fade with curly top, slim athletic silhouette, sharp facial acting.
* **Match Wardrobe:** Plain white ribbed athletic tank top (or faded burgundy tee), royal blue sport shorts with white piping, worn canvas sneakers or bare feet with taped toes.

#### 2. AYUK (Playmaker / Zino's Close Partner)
* **Status:** New Character Turnaround Model.
* **Neighborhood:** Lives 1 house north of Zino's compound. Father later owns a TV.
* **Physique & Face:** Wiry, agile, slightly shorter than Zino, narrow oval face, high cheekbones, bright observant eyes, short buzz cut (taper fade).
* **Match Wardrobe:** Forest green athletic sleeveless singlet, white knee-above sport shorts with green side stripe, bare feet with dusty ankles.

#### 3. BISMARK (Midfield Anchor)
* **Status:** New Character Turnaround Model.
* **Neighborhood:** Lives south of the compound, adjacent to the TV house.
* **Physique & Face:** Sturdy, broad-chested, slightly darker rich brown skin tone, squared jawline, calm and steady expression, neat low crop fade.
* **Match Wardrobe:** Deep royal blue athletic tank top, dark blue running shorts, worn dusty brown sneakers.

### TEAM 2: AREY'S SIDE
*Colors / Silhouette: Red, Yellow & Orange trims*

#### 4. AREY (Opposing Team Captain)
* **Status:** New Character Turnaround Model.
* **Neighborhood:** Ayuk's cousin, lives in Fiango neighborhood.
* **Physique & Face:** Athletic, broad-shouldered, strong jaw, competitive and fiery gaze, low textured afro cut with natural hairline.
* **Match Wardrobe:** Bold red athletic singlet/tank top, black sport shorts with red piping, scuffed grey trainers.

#### 5. NOGE (Aggressive Defender / Hard Tackler)
* **Status:** New Character Turnaround Model.
* **Neighborhood:** Lives directly next to Ayuk's house.
* **Physique & Face:** Stocky, powerhouse build, thick neck, rounder determined face, close buzz cut. Fearless on the dirt.
* **Match Wardrobe:** Vibrant yellow athletic short-sleeve t-shirt, navy blue athletic shorts, bare dusty feet with taped left ankle (the sliding tackle foot).

#### 6. ROBI (Speed Winger)
* **Status:** New Character Turnaround Model.
* **Neighborhood:** Lives next to Bismark.
* **Physique & Face:** Lean, lanky, long legs, expressive and animated face, slightly wavy low crop. Quick feet on the wings.
* **Match Wardrobe:** Faded orange lightweight t-shirt, charcoal athletic shorts, worn blue canvas sneakers.

### SIDELINE NON-PLAYERS (SPECTATORS / OBSERVERS)
* **Gideon:** Sits on a wooden crate / roadside bench on the verge, holding his camera bag or chewing a stick. Wears green polo and dark-brown trousers. Banters and laughs. DOES NOT PLAY.
* **Luc:** Walks along the roadside verge carrying his small pocket Bible. Wears mustard t-shirt and loose navy casual trousers. Steps in to preach peace when fights break out. DOES NOT PLAY.

### NEIGHBOURHOOD FIGURE: GRAND NESTOR
* **Status:** Established in Episode 6 "Lunch Money" (storyboard v4 shots N00, N01, N02). Confirmed by the author 17 Nov 2026.
* **Who he is:** One of the neighbourhood older boys who always stands next to the convenience store and knows all the latest gist in town. He occasionally takes care of bets (holds the cote) and settles disputes among the youths of the neighbourhood. Addressed as "Grand Nestor" or "Grand".
* **Look (as in Episode 6):** Young adult, red cap worn backwards, red plaid shirt open over a white tee. Stands by the small corner/convenience store kiosk on the Fiango road.
* **Role in Episode 7 "First Bottle":** Holds the 600 CFA pot, stops the fight, rules that Zino's team won the disputed goal and hands the money to Zino.
* **Voice:** Recorded by the author; keep the same voice in every episode.
