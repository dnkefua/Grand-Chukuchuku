## Added on 28 October 2026

- Zino's father died after a snake bite while spraying cocoa when Zino was two. In the family account, he cut the bite area and tried to suck out the venom, then passed out before reaching home and died with his right arm swollen. Zino has no direct memory of the death; it should enter through family testimony or a prologue, not a firsthand flashback.
- Zino's mother was a primary-school teacher from a young age and throughout his childhood.
- Gideon is the correct spelling. He is sharp and business-minded, bought his first camera in secondary school, often paid when he and Zino went out, and valued Zino's help speaking with girls.
- Luc is God-fearing, carries a small Gideons Bible, preaches to students, and values Zino's honesty, humour, mathematics help, and conversations about girls.
- The Fiango compound holds two families, three single mothers, two bachelors, and three student tenants. The landlord collects monthly rent and electricity money.
- Zino reports witnessing at least four incidents of jungle justice in Fiango against people accused of theft. The hard-R master will depict this brutality directly through Zino's secondary-school perspective, while preserving the uncertainty of the accusation and the consequences for everyone involved.
- Professional School is a real daytime vocational school for building, construction, and electrical training. Around June in the secondary-school years, its classrooms open at night for exam-period study. Some students study; others socialize, smoke, drink, pursue relationships, or use the session as a reason to leave home. Do not treat this as true of every student.
- The first five secondary-school years are day school with Luc and Gideon. The final two years are high school at a remote boarding school reached after a day of travel through mountains and valleys. It has top students, wealthy parents, white teachers and priests, strict routine, bullying, and severe discipline.
- The earlier statement that Zino jumped a boarding-school fence to eat at home is corrected. He witnessed students punished after jumping the fence to buy food; he did not jump himself.
- At the end of five day-school years, Zino writes ten GCE Ordinary-Level subjects out of a maximum eleven. After two boarding years, he writes five GCE Advanced-Level subjects, the maximum and his university study area. Results are read on the radio. The boarding school has a 100% Advanced-Level pass rate, so Zino waits for his name with excitement rather than fear of failing.
- Proud of the Ordinary-Level result, Zino's uncle promises that Zino can choose any school and go to America if he succeeds in all five Advanced-Level subjects. Zino chooses boarding school. After the A Levels, the uncle defers America until Zino completes a first university degree.
- Football is an everyday Fiango rhythm: players contribute small stakes before street matches, and the winning side takes the pooled money. Matches happen in the evenings on the road, with slippers sometimes used as goalposts.
- “Nacking Bottle” is a street-football variation: a beer bottle stands at each end and the first side to strike the other side's bottle three times wins. Zino saved lunch money to play. The exact ages, stakes, opponents and individual matches remain to be recorded.
- At school, Zino admires and talks to girls, sometimes to help the quieter Gideon start a conversation. He also helps Luc with mathematics while Luc continues approaching students to preach.
- Gideon pursues camera work and books or seeks birthday-party gigs. The exact camera, event dates, clients and payments remain open.
