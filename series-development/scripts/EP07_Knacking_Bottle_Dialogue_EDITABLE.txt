GRAND-CHUKUCHUKU - EPISODE 07 - "KNACKING BOTTLE" (WORKING TITLE)
STATUS: DRAFT v2 for author edit. Not yet storyboarded. Pidgin lines to be recorded by the author.
SETTING: Fiango dirt road outside the compound, hot afternoon, red dust. Look: 3D cinematic (Blender base).
RUNTIME TARGET: about 2:15-2:30
RULES OF THE GAME: two 33 Export bottles stand 15 m apart. Knock the other team's bottle down with the ball = one goal. No offside. First team to three goals wins the 600 CFA pot (6 x 100 CFA, held in a tin cup off the pitch).
TEAMS: ZINO'S SIDE (Zino, Ayuk, Bismark) - TAICHI | AREY'S SIDE (Arey, Noge, Robi) - KUNG FU  (assumption: author may swap)
ARRIVES AT THE END: GRAND NESTOR - respected older "grand" of Fiango, the elder whose ruling settles disputes on the road. Appearance and voice TO BE DEFINED BY AUTHOR (not yet in the character bible).
SIDELINE: Gideon (crate, chewing a stick, does not play), Luc (pocket Bible, does not play)
INJURIES TO TRACK: Ayuk - stubbed, bruised toes (scene 3). Zino - scraped, bruised right knee and shin, bruised bare feet (scene 6 onward). Noge - taped left ankle.

SCENE 1 - THE COTE (0:00-0:15)
Low angle, 33 Export bottle in foreground; whip-pan to the second bottle 15 m away. Coins in a tin cup on Gideon's crate.
GIDEON: You sure say your 100 francs want play today, Zino? Bottle no dey get mercy oh. Once goal enter, no refund!
ZINO: My 100 francs get ambition. Six of we don put money. Winning team carry 600, each man take 200. Three goals.
AREY: Talk plenty, Zino. Today your knee go kiss gravel.
LUC: Eh! Na only game o. Make nobody turn am to war.

SCENE 2 - NO OFFSIDE (0:15-0:30)
Kickoff in a blast of dust. Arey stalks behind Zino's bottle; Ayuk guards it low like a goalkeeper.
AREY: Pass am behind! Bottle no get front or back!
AYUK: Zino, watch your rear! Cover the glass!
BISMARK: I get am! Hold your line!

SCENE 3 - ROBI'S RUN, NOGE'S FIRST SLIDE (0:30-0:45)
Robi flies down the wing with double step-overs. Ayuk blocks; Noge slides in and takes Ayuk's feet.
ROBI: Dem no fit catch me!
AYUK: (NO WORDS - only a pained moan through clenched teeth. The visuals carry it: he hops on one foot, grips his foot, close-up of the swollen, bruised toes.)
NOGE: Na football, Ayuk. Stand up.

SCENE 4 - AREY'S GOAL (0:45-0:55)
Cross from Robi. Arey, behind the bottle, taps it. CLINK, the bottle topples.
AREY: ONE GOAL!
GIDEON: Arey one, Zino zero! Zino, your bottle dey cry o!

SCENE 5 - THE DRIBBLE (0:55-1:10)
Zino wins the ball with his chest, turns, elastico past Robi, rainbow flick over Noge.
ZINO: Ayuk, open for me! Bismark, cover!
ROBI: Wait! Where e pass?!
GIDEON: Chai! This boy get leg pass leg!

SCENE 6 - THE BLOODY SLIDE (1:10-1:20)
Zino winds up to shoot. Noge slides across the gravel and clips him. Close-up: scraped knee and shin, bruising, blood beading in the dust. Zino gets up.
ZINO: You come play ball or you come scrape my skin?!
NOGE: Na gravel dey judge, no be me!

SCENE 7 - ZINO'S GOAL (1:20-1:30)
Ayuk squares it. Zino fakes left, spins, low strike. Slow motion: ball clips the bottle's neck, CLINK-CLACK, it topples.
ZINO: ONE GOAL DON ENTER! One-one!
AYUK: (limping, grinning) Na so we dey do!

SCENE 8 - THE MONTAGE (1:30-1:38)
Quick cuts: nutmegs, a header, a two-footed slide by Bismark, bruised feet slapping the dirt. Tally marks scratched in the dust: 2-2. Everyone is bleeding or bruised.
GIDEON: Two-two! Next goal carry 600!

SCENE 9 - THE DISPUTED GOAL (1:38-1:50)
Zino, limping on his bruised knee, strikes. The ball skips off a stone and clips the bottle. It falls.
ZINO: GOAL! THREE! The bottle sing like church bell! Bring the 600!
AREY: Which goal?! E touch stone for ground before bottle! That one no count!
GIDEON: (honest, scratching his head) Bottle sing... but stone talk too. I no fit lie.
LUC: Blessed are the peacemakers! No turn 100 francs to war!
AREY: Comot for road, Pastor!

SCENE 10 - THE FIGHT: TAICHI VS KUNG FU (1:50-2:20)
Arey throws a hook. Zino sways under it with a slow Taichi turn. Teams split by style: Zino, Ayuk and Bismark use soft, circular Taichi (cloud hands, push hands, redirecting force); Arey, Noge and Robi use fast kung fu (tiger claws, spinning kicks, flying kicks). Dust clouds, wide shots with the whole road and both teams visible at all times. Zino fights on a bruised knee, so he keeps his steps small and slow.
ZINO: (calm, circling) Your power too plenty. Make I borrow am.
AREY: Which kind dance be this?!
ZINO: Na old man style. Your own force dey come back to you.
NOGE: Kung fu go finish una!
BISMARK: Una dey blow wind, no be fire.
GIDEON: (up on his crate) Bruce Lee meet Grandpa for Fiango road!
Finale: Arey launches a flying kick; Zino turns the force, Arey sails past and lands flat on his back in the dust.
NESTOR: (O.S., deep, calm, carrying voice) ENOUGH!
Everyone freezes mid-strike.

SCENE 11 - GRAND NESTOR DECIDES (2:20-2:40)
Grand Nestor walks onto the road through the settling dust. He looks at the fallen bottle, the stone, the boys' bruises.
NESTOR: Una dey fight for 600 francs for my road? Who go talk wetin happen?
AREY: (sitting up) Grand Nestor, the ball touch stone before bottle!
ZINO: Bottle fall, Grand. I no lie.
NESTOR: (studies the stone and the fallen bottle) The road na the pitch. The stone dey for the pitch. The bottle don fall. Na goal. Zino don win.
Nestor takes the tin cup from Gideon and hands the money to Zino.
NESTOR: Take your money. Share am well. And Arey, football no be fight. Go clean your mouth.
AREY: (head down) Yes, Grand Nestor.
ZINO: Thank you, Grand Nestor. Two hundred for Ayuk, two hundred for Bismark, two hundred for me.
GIDEON: Chai! Champion with bloody knee!
END CARD: EPISODE 08
