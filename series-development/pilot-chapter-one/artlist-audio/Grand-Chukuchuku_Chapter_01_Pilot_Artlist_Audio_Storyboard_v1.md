## Music identity

Fiango must sound lived in before it sounds cinematic. Begin with compound life—bucket rope, radio through walls, bare feet and a football on packed earth. Music arrives as an emotional extension of Zino’s storytelling, not as a generic “African” backdrop. The main musical contrast is:

- **Fiango bed:** warm, percussive, guitar-led instrumental Afro-fusion/highlife-leaning groove. It should support Pidgin dialogue rather than compete with it.
- **Grand motif:** a dignified navy-and-gold lift: muted electric guitar or clean plucked strings, a small bell/chime, and one controlled upward phrase. It is elegant, not heroic-action music.
- **Money pressure:** remove melody and low end before adding darkness. Use bucket rope, notebook paper, a distant radio and empty space.
- **Nacking Bottle:** dry foot percussion, handclaps and a single rhythmic low pulse. Glass hits are story beats, not decoration.
- **Fee notice:** no music under Mother’s last lines. The final unresolved Grand motif begins only after Zino opens his hand.

## Mix and delivery notes

1. Record dialogue first. Build the music edit around the recorded pace; do not force performances to fit a chosen song.
2. Keep music at least 8–12 dB below dialogue during Pidgin and English lines. Test subtitles against the quiet sequences, especially Boards 15–17.
3. Use no licensed track beneath the school-fee conversation until the final title lift. Silence is part of the score.
4. Download and archive the selected Artlist licence record, exact track title, artist, version, licence date and project usage beside the final edit. Do not identify an Artlist track as cleared until the account holder confirms it.
5. Export a 5:00 master, plus 15-second and 60-second cutdowns that preserve the same B1/E1 motif.

## Music supervisor handoff

When Artlist access is available, audition three candidates for each of A–E. Score each candidate against: dialogue space, warmth, rhythmic fit, editability, cultural specificity without false claims, and the ability to end unresolved. Select one coherent family rather than five unrelated songs.
