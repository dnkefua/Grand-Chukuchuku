# Mirabel and Lizzy — recognition at a distance

**Date:** 30 October 2026.

**Source:** the author rejected the first pair as almost identical, then confirmed that both are Black girls with low-cut hair. The subsequent invented bob and puffs are therefore superseded. Low-cut natural hair is a character requirement, not an optional recognition device.

## What was wrong

Both figures used nearly identical facial proportions, braided-back hairstyles ending near the nape, similar heights and comparable skirt/dress outlines. A low bun versus a low puff and different shirt colours did not provide dependable recognition in a wide shot. The earlier review gave too much weight to those small differences.

The first redesign solved the hair-silhouette difference by inventing a bob and two high puffs. That conflicted with the author's low-cut correction. The current pair preserves low natural crops and distinguishes them through facial construction, proportions and casual garment outlines.

Black identity and low-cut hair are author-confirmed. Exact crop styling, facial construction, relative proportions and garments remain animation proposals. Both girls retain low cuts with the sky-blue shirt/dark-blue skirt daytime uniform. The trousers and gold skirt belong to casual model proposals. Facial/head-shape and proportion differences must carry into uniformed scenes; that matching-uniform lineup still needs its own layout drawing and review.
